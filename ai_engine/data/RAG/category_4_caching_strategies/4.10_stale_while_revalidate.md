# Stale-While-Revalidate


## chunk_id
- 4.10

## category
- caching_strategies

## type
- caching_strategy

## definition
- serving slightly stale cached responses while refreshing them asynchronously to reduce latency and avoid stampedes.

## when_to_use
- read-heavy endpoints where slight staleness is acceptable
- hot keys where refresh work is expensive
- reducing tail latency during cache refresh cycles

## when_not_to_use
- data requiring strict freshness and strong consistency
- when background refresh cannot be made reliable and observable

## decision_signals
- need: read-heavy endpoints where slight staleness is acceptable
- need: hot keys where refresh work is expensive
- willing to accept: serves stale data by design and must be acceptable to users

## advantages
- improves p95 latency by avoiding synchronous recomputation
- reduces origin load by smoothing refresh traffic

## disadvantages
- serves stale data by design and must be acceptable to users
- refresh failures can extend staleness without good monitoring

## anti_patterns
- using this when data requiring strict freshness and strong consistency
- use this when background refresh cannot be made reliable and observable
- ignoring the downside that serves stale data by design and must be acceptable to users

## real_world_examples
- serving cached catalog pages while refreshing in the background
- cdn stale serving with background revalidation
