# Cache Warming


## chunk_id
- 4.11

## category
- caching_strategies

## type
- caching_strategy

## definition
- pre-populating cache entries before traffic hits to reduce cold-start misses and latency spikes.

## when_to_use
- predictable hot keys like top products or homepage content
- deployments and restarts that would otherwise cause cold cache periods
- large caches where warmup time materially affects user experience

## when_not_to_use
- when hot keys are unpredictable or change too rapidly
- when warmup would overload the origin or exceed rate limits

## decision_signals
- need: predictable hot keys like top products or homepage content
- need: deployments and restarts that would otherwise cause cold cache periods
- willing to accept: can waste cache space on items that are not actually hot

## advantages
- reduces latency spikes after deploys and cache flushes
- improves early hit rate and stabilizes origin load

## disadvantages
- can waste cache space on items that are not actually hot
- warmup jobs can create load spikes if not throttled

## anti_patterns
- use this when hot keys are unpredictable or change too rapidly
- use this when warmup would overload the origin or exceed rate limits
- ignoring the downside that can waste cache space on items that are not actually hot

## real_world_examples
- warming catalog and pricing keys after a deployment
- precomputing and caching top feed items periodically
