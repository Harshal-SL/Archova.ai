# Health Checks and Readiness


## chunk_id
- 8.15

## category
- deployment_strategies

## type
- deployment_strategy

## definition
- using health and readiness signals to ensure only healthy instances receive traffic during deploys and steady state.

## when_to_use
- load-balanced services and rolling updates
- preventing traffic to instances still warming up
- safe autoscaling and incident mitigation

## when_not_to_use
- when checks are unreliable and cause flapping without fixes
- when the service cannot accurately detect dependency readiness

## decision_signals
- need: load-balanced services and rolling updates
- need: preventing traffic to instances still warming up
- willing to accept: poorly designed checks can cause cascading outages

## advantages
- reduces user impact during deployments
- improves resilience by routing away from unhealthy instances

## disadvantages
- poorly designed checks can cause cascading outages
- checks can become expensive if they test too much

## anti_patterns
- use this when checks are unreliable and cause flapping without fixes
- use this when the service cannot accurately detect dependency readiness
- ignoring the downside that poorly designed checks can cause cascading outages

## real_world_examples
- readiness probe that confirms dependencies are reachable and caches are warmed
- liveness probe that restarts stuck processes without taking down healthy nodes
