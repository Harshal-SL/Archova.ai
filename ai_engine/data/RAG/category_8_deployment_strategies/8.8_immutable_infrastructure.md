# Immutable Infrastructure


## chunk_id
- 8.8

## category
- deployment_strategies

## type
- deployment_strategy

## definition
- deploying by replacing servers or containers rather than modifying them in place, ensuring environments are reproducible.

## when_to_use
- reducing configuration drift across environments
- improving rollback by switching to known-good artifacts
- standardizing deployments for many services

## when_not_to_use
- legacy systems that require in-place updates and cannot be rebuilt
- highly stateful nodes where replacement is risky without safe replication

## decision_signals
- need: reducing configuration drift across environments
- need: improving rollback by switching to known-good artifacts
- willing to accept: can increase deployment time due to rebuild and replace

## advantages
- improves consistency and auditability of deployments
- simplifies rollback by replacing with prior artifacts

## disadvantages
- can increase deployment time due to rebuild and replace
- requires good automation and image build pipelines

## anti_patterns
- using this when legacy systems that require in-place updates and cannot be rebuilt
- using this when highly stateful nodes where replacement is risky without safe replication
- ignoring the downside that can increase deployment time due to rebuild and replace

## real_world_examples
- deploying new vm images instead of patching running machines
- rolling out new containers with a new image digest and configuration
