# Environment Promotion


## chunk_id
- 8.12

## category
- deployment_strategies

## type
- deployment_strategy

## definition
- moving the same versioned artifact through environments (dev, staging, prod) with controlled approvals and checks.

## when_to_use
- reducing risk by validating changes in production-like stages
- meeting compliance requirements for approvals and segregation of duties
- coordinating releases across multiple services

## when_not_to_use
- when environments differ significantly and promotion would not validate reality
- when hotfixes must bypass stages without adding compensating controls

## decision_signals
- need: reducing risk by validating changes in production-like stages
- need: meeting compliance requirements for approvals and segregation of duties
- willing to accept: can slow delivery if environments and approvals are heavy

## advantages
- improves confidence by validating the same artifact progressively
- reduces drift between environments when promotion is consistent

## disadvantages
- can slow delivery if environments and approvals are heavy
- environment differences can create false confidence

## anti_patterns
- use this when environments differ significantly and promotion would not validate reality
- use this when hotfixes must bypass stages without adding compensating controls
- ignoring the downside that can slow delivery if environments and approvals are heavy

## real_world_examples
- promoting an image digest from staging to production after passing canary analysis
- requiring approval for production promotion but not for dev deployments
