# Automated Rollbacks


## chunk_id
- 8.16

## category
- deployment_strategies

## type
- deployment_strategy

## definition
- reverting or aborting deployments automatically when health metrics cross defined failure thresholds.

## when_to_use
- canary and progressive delivery where fast mitigation is required
- systems with clear health signals and slos
- reducing mean time to recovery during bad releases

## when_not_to_use
- when metrics are noisy and would cause frequent false rollbacks
- when rollback itself is risky due to irreversible data changes

## decision_signals
- need: canary and progressive delivery where fast mitigation is required
- need: systems with clear health signals and slos
- willing to accept: requires well-defined thresholds and reliable telemetry

## advantages
- limits blast radius and shortens incident duration
- improves confidence to ship frequently

## disadvantages
- requires well-defined thresholds and reliable telemetry
- can hide underlying issues if rollbacks happen silently without follow-up

## anti_patterns
- use this when metrics are noisy and would cause frequent false rollbacks
- use this when rollback itself is risky due to irreversible data changes
- ignoring the downside that requires well-defined thresholds and reliable telemetry

## real_world_examples
- aborting a canary when error rate increases beyond a threshold
- rolling back a deployment when latency p95 exceeds the slo budget
