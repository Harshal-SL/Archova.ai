# Compliance Change Management


## chunk_id
- 8.24

## category
- deployment_strategies

## type
- deployment_strategy

## definition
- managing deployments with approvals, audit trails, and separation of duties to meet regulatory and organizational controls.

## when_to_use
- regulated industries requiring formal change processes
- production environments with strict access and approval requirements
- demonstrating auditability for infrastructure and application changes

## when_not_to_use
- low-risk internal tools where process overhead would block delivery
- when emergency fixes require a faster path with compensating controls

## decision_signals
- need: regulated industries requiring formal change processes
- need: production environments with strict access and approval requirements
- willing to accept: can slow delivery and increase coordination overhead

## advantages
- improves accountability and reduces unauthorized changes
- provides clear audit trails for incidents and reviews

## disadvantages
- can slow delivery and increase coordination overhead
- heavy processes can incentivize bypasses if not designed well

## anti_patterns
- using this when low-risk internal tools where process overhead would block delivery
- use this when emergency fixes require a faster path with compensating controls
- ignoring the downside that can slow delivery and increase coordination overhead

## real_world_examples
- requiring approvals for production promotion while logging who approved and why
- separating roles for code authors and production deploy approvers
