# Multi-Region Failover Drills


## chunk_id
- 8.22

## category
- deployment_strategies

## type
- deployment_strategy

## definition
- regularly practicing regional failover and recovery procedures to validate rto, rpo, and operational readiness.

## when_to_use
- systems requiring high availability across regions
- validating disaster recovery and incident response processes
- ensuring teams can execute failover under pressure

## when_not_to_use
- systems without multi-region architecture or clear failover ownership
- when failover would risk data corruption without safeguards

## decision_signals
- need: systems requiring high availability across regions
- need: validating disaster recovery and incident response processes
- willing to accept: drills can disrupt service if poorly planned

## advantages
- uncovers gaps in documentation, tooling, and dependencies
- improves confidence in disaster recovery capabilities

## disadvantages
- drills can disrupt service if poorly planned
- can be expensive due to running active-active or warm standby capacity

## anti_patterns
- using this when systems without multi-region architecture or clear failover ownership
- use this when failover would risk data corruption without safeguards
- ignoring the downside that drills can disrupt service if poorly planned

## real_world_examples
- failing over a read-heavy service to a secondary region and measuring impact
- validating database replication lag and cutover steps during a planned drill
