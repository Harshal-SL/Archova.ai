# Recommendation System

## chunk_id
- 12.22


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- a system that suggests items to users using historical interactions, context, and ranking models, often under strict latency constraints.

## when_to_use
- personalizing feeds, catalogs, playlists, or next-best actions
- combining batch training with online serving
- improving engagement and conversion through ranking

## when_not_to_use
- small catalogs where manual curation is sufficient
- when personalization risks outweigh benefits due to privacy or bias concerns

## decision_signals
- need: personalizing feeds, catalogs, playlists, or next-best actions
- need: combining batch training with online serving
- willing to accept: requires high-quality event instrumentation and feedback loops

## advantages
- increases relevance and user engagement
- supports multiple surfaces (home, search reranking, email)

## disadvantages
- requires high-quality event instrumentation and feedback loops
- risks bias, filter bubbles, and cold-start problems

## anti_patterns
- using this when small catalogs where manual curation is sufficient
- use this when personalization risks outweigh benefits due to privacy or bias concerns
- ignoring the downside that requires high-quality event instrumentation and feedback loops

## real_world_examples
- offline training jobs producing candidate sets and embeddings
- online ranking service combining candidates, rules, and model scores
