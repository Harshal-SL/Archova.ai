# Analytics Data Warehouse

## chunk_id
- 12.45


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- a system that ingests business events into a warehouse or lakehouse, supports transformations, and enables dashboards and ad hoc analysis.

## when_to_use
- separating analytical workloads from operational databases
- building reliable etl/elt pipelines with lineage and quality checks
- supporting long-term retention and historical analysis

## when_not_to_use
- low-volume reporting where operational queries are sufficient
- when data governance and ownership cannot be defined

## decision_signals
- need: separating analytical workloads from operational databases
- need: building reliable etl/elt pipelines with lineage and quality checks
- willing to accept: data freshness is often eventual and requires clear expectations

## advantages
- improves performance and stability of operational systems
- supports standardized metrics and repeatable transformations

## disadvantages
- data freshness is often eventual and requires clear expectations
- pipeline failures can silently skew business decisions

## anti_patterns
- using this when low-volume reporting where operational queries are sufficient
- use this when data governance and ownership cannot be defined
- ignoring the downside that data freshness is often eventual and requires clear expectations

## real_world_examples
- event ingestion into a lake with batch transformations into curated tables
- data quality rules that fail builds when key metrics drift unexpectedly
