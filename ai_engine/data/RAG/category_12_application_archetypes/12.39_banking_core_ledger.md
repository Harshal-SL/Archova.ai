# Banking Core Ledger

## chunk_id
- 12.39


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- a financial system that records accounts and transactions with strict correctness, auditability, and regulatory controls.

## when_to_use
- modeling append-only ledgers and reconciliation workflows
- designing strong authorization, approvals, and segregation of duties
- planning disaster recovery and tamper-evident audit trails

## when_not_to_use
- non-financial systems without money movement or regulated audits
- prototypes where correctness and auditability are not required

## decision_signals
- need: modeling append-only ledgers and reconciliation workflows
- need: designing strong authorization, approvals, and segregation of duties
- willing to accept: changes require careful governance and controlled releases

## advantages
- clear invariants and audit needs drive robust data modeling
- supports strong traceability for investigations and compliance

## disadvantages
- changes require careful governance and controlled releases
- performance optimization must preserve correctness guarantees

## anti_patterns
- using this when non-financial systems without money movement or regulated audits
- using this when prototypes where correctness and auditability are not required
- ignoring the downside that changes require careful governance and controlled releases

## real_world_examples
- double-entry ledger postings with immutable transaction records
- daily reconciliation with external statements and exception handling
