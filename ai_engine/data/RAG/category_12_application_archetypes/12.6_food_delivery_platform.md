# Food Delivery Platform

## chunk_id
- 12.6


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- a multi-sided marketplace connecting customers, restaurants, couriers, and order workflows with real-time tracking and coordination.

## when_to_use
- modeling dispatch, routing, and order orchestration
- designing cancellation and refund workflows with compensations
- handling peak demand and regional operational constraints

## when_not_to_use
- simple retail delivery without live courier dispatch
- systems that do not require near-real-time tracking

## decision_signals
- need: modeling dispatch, routing, and order orchestration
- need: designing cancellation and refund workflows with compensations
- willing to accept: complex cancellation edge cases and partial failures

## advantages
- clear workflow stages for sagas and state machines
- benefits from event-driven updates to customers and restaurants

## disadvantages
- complex cancellation edge cases and partial failures
- strong coupling between time constraints, inventory, and courier availability

## anti_patterns
- using this when simple retail delivery without live courier dispatch
- using this when systems that do not require near-real-time tracking
- ignoring the downside that complex cancellation edge cases and partial failures

## real_world_examples
- restaurant accepts order, courier assigned, pickup confirmed, delivery completed
- refund and redelivery compensation flows after courier cancellation
