# CRUD Web App

## chunk_id
- 12.1


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- a standard create/read/update/delete application with forms, validation, role-based access, and transactional persistence.

## when_to_use
- internal tools and business apps with structured data
- back-office workflows requiring audit trails
- systems where correctness matters more than extreme scale

## when_not_to_use
- high-throughput event ingestion requiring streaming-first design
- systems needing complex offline sync across devices

## decision_signals
- need: internal tools and business apps with structured data
- need: back-office workflows requiring audit trails
- willing to accept: can become a monolith with tangled business rules

## advantages
- straightforward domain modeling and transactions
- simple to test and reason about

## disadvantages
- can become a monolith with tangled business rules
- search and reporting workloads can overload the primary database

## anti_patterns
- using this when high-throughput event ingestion requiring streaming-first design
- using this when systems needing complex offline sync across devices
- ignoring the downside that can become a monolith with tangled business rules

## real_world_examples
- inventory management dashboards
- hr systems for employee profiles and approvals
