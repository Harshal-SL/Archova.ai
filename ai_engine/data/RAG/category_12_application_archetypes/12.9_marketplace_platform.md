# Marketplace Platform

## chunk_id
- 12.9


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- a multi-sided platform that matches supply and demand, manages listings, transactions, trust and safety, and dispute workflows.

## when_to_use
- enabling third-party sellers/providers and buyers
- modeling search, ranking, and transactional flows with escrow or payouts
- implementing fraud, reviews, and dispute resolution

## when_not_to_use
- single-vendor commerce without third-party participants
- simple classifieds with no payments or fulfillment

## decision_signals
- need: enabling third-party sellers/providers and buyers
- need: modeling search, ranking, and transactional flows with escrow or payouts
- willing to accept: trust and safety introduces complex abuse detection and moderation

## advantages
- clear domains (catalog, trust, payments, fulfillment) support modularization
- event-driven workflows fit disputes and payouts well

## disadvantages
- trust and safety introduces complex abuse detection and moderation
- identity, payments, and compliance requirements increase effort

## anti_patterns
- using this when single-vendor commerce without third-party participants
- using this when simple classifieds with no payments or fulfillment
- ignoring the downside that trust and safety introduces complex abuse detection and moderation

## real_world_examples
- gig marketplaces with payouts and ratings
- online marketplaces with seller onboarding and dispute handling
