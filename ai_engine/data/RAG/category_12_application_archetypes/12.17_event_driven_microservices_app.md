# Event Driven Microservices App

## chunk_id
- 12.17


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- an application decomposed into services that communicate primarily through events, enabling loose coupling and asynchronous workflows.

## when_to_use
- complex domains with many integrations and workflows
- needing independent scaling and deployment of components
- capturing event history for projections and analytics

## when_not_to_use
- small teams and simple domains where a monolith is sufficient
- use cases requiring strict, immediate consistency across domains

## decision_signals
- need: complex domains with many integrations and workflows
- need: needing independent scaling and deployment of components
- willing to accept: eventual consistency and debugging distributed flows are hard

## advantages
- reduces coupling and supports scalable, resilient workflows
- enables multiple downstream consumers without changing producers

## disadvantages
- eventual consistency and debugging distributed flows are hard
- requires strong observability and schema/version management

## anti_patterns
- using this when small teams and simple domains where a monolith is sufficient
- using this when use cases requiring strict, immediate consistency across domains
- ignoring the downside that eventual consistency and debugging distributed flows are hard

## real_world_examples
- order events driving fulfillment, billing, and notifications
- user events feeding personalization and analytics pipelines
