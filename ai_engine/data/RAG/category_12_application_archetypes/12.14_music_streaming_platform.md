# Music Streaming Platform

## chunk_id
- 12.14


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- a platform that serves audio content with personalized playlists, search, licensing controls, and low-latency playback.

## when_to_use
- modeling personalization and playlist generation pipelines
- designing search and catalog services with caching
- handling global delivery with regional licensing constraints

## when_not_to_use
- static audio hosting without personalization or catalog search
- small catalogs where a single database can handle discovery

## decision_signals
- need: modeling personalization and playlist generation pipelines
- need: designing search and catalog services with caching
- willing to accept: complex licensing rules and regional catalogs

## advantages
- separates catalog, search, playback, and personalization services
- benefits from caching and precomputed recommendation read models

## disadvantages
- complex licensing rules and regional catalogs
- personalization pipelines require high-quality event data and feedback loops

## anti_patterns
- using this when static audio hosting without personalization or catalog search
- using this when small catalogs where a single database can handle discovery
- ignoring the downside that complex licensing rules and regional catalogs

## real_world_examples
- generating daily mixes from listening events and storing as materialized playlists
- using cdn caching for audio segments and artwork
