# Customer Support Platform

## chunk_id
- 12.47


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- a system for managing customer tickets, conversations, routing, knowledge base articles, and agent workflows across channels.

## when_to_use
- designing workflow-driven systems with queues and assignments
- integrating email, chat, and call center tooling
- building reporting on resolution times and satisfaction metrics

## when_not_to_use
- small support teams where off-the-shelf tools meet all needs
- products with minimal user support requirements

## decision_signals
- need: designing workflow-driven systems with queues and assignments
- need: integrating email, chat, and call center tooling
- willing to accept: workflow customization can become complex and brittle

## advantages
- clear domain model (ticket, conversation, assignment, sla)
- benefits from event-driven updates and audit trails

## disadvantages
- workflow customization can become complex and brittle
- permissions and pii handling are critical and easy to get wrong

## anti_patterns
- using this when small support teams where off-the-shelf tools meet all needs
- using this when products with minimal user support requirements
- ignoring the downside that workflow customization can become complex and brittle

## real_world_examples
- routing tickets by topic and priority with reassignment rules
- auditing agent actions and masking sensitive data in transcripts
