# Spotify Music Streaming Platform

## chunk_id
- 12.53


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- an audio streaming system emphasizing fast playback, playlist personalization, search, and licensing-aware catalog delivery.

## when_to_use
- modeling playlist generation and recommendation pipelines
- designing search and discovery over a large catalog
- building global playback with caching and cdn delivery

## when_not_to_use
- small catalogs without personalization or search requirements
- systems where licensing and regional catalogs are irrelevant

## decision_signals
- personalized playlists require offline training plus online ranking
- playback paths need low startup latency (<1s) and high availability
- catalog/search updates are frequent and must reindex reliably

## advantages
- clear separation between playback delivery and personalization computation
- projection-based search supports low-latency discovery

## disadvantages
- personalization requires strong event instrumentation and drift monitoring
- licensing constraints complicate catalog consistency and caching

## anti_patterns
- running heavy ranking computations in the playback request path
- serving personalization without evaluation and guardrails
- mixing catalog writes with search reads without projections

## real_world_examples
- daily mix playlists materialized from listening events
- cdn delivery for audio segments and artwork
