# Ticketing And Booking System

## chunk_id
- 12.28


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- a system that sells seats or time slots with inventory holds, payments, confirmations, and strict fairness under high contention.

## when_to_use
- designing reservation holds and expiration strategies
- modeling high-contention inventory and anti-oversell protections
- handling refunds, cancellations, and rebooking workflows

## when_not_to_use
- unlimited inventory purchases with no contention
- offline scheduling where concurrency is low

## decision_signals
- need: designing reservation holds and expiration strategies
- need: modeling high-contention inventory and anti-oversell protections
- willing to accept: complex edge cases around holds, payment failures, and timeouts

## advantages
- clear need for idempotency and consistency around inventory
- benefits from queueing and rate limits during high demand

## disadvantages
- complex edge cases around holds, payment failures, and timeouts
- fairness and bot protection add additional requirements

## anti_patterns
- using this when unlimited inventory purchases with no contention
- using this when offline scheduling where concurrency is low
- ignoring the downside that complex edge cases around holds, payment failures, and timeouts

## real_world_examples
- short-lived seat holds with automatic expiration
- queue-based throttling during major event ticket drops
