# Payment Processing System

## chunk_id
- 12.15


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- a system that authorizes, captures, refunds, and reconciles payments while enforcing strong correctness, security, and audit trails.

## when_to_use
- designing money movement workflows with strong idempotency
- integrating with external payment processors and banking rails
- implementing fraud checks and ledger reconciliation

## when_not_to_use
- systems without financial side effects
- prototypes where payment is handled entirely by a third-party hosted checkout

## decision_signals
- need: designing money movement workflows with strong idempotency
- need: integrating with external payment processors and banking rails
- willing to accept: external dependencies and retries create complex failure handling

## advantages
- clear invariants and audit requirements drive robust design
- benefits from idempotency and strong observability

## disadvantages
- external dependencies and retries create complex failure handling
- compliance and security requirements increase operational burden

## anti_patterns
- using this when systems without financial side effects
- using this when prototypes where payment is handled entirely by a third-party hosted checkout
- ignoring the downside that external dependencies and retries create complex failure handling

## real_world_examples
- idempotent payment capture with dedupe keys and retry-safe handlers
- reconciliation jobs comparing processor reports with internal ledgers
