# Subscription Billing App

## chunk_id
- 12.27


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- an application that manages plans, entitlements, invoicing, payments, proration, and renewals with auditability and correctness.

## when_to_use
- saas monetization with recurring billing
- enforcing feature entitlements based on subscription state
- handling dunning, retries, and payment method updates

## when_not_to_use
- one-time purchases with no recurring charges
- products delegating all billing and entitlements to a third-party with no internal logic

## decision_signals
- need: saas monetization with recurring billing
- need: enforcing feature entitlements based on subscription state
- willing to accept: edge cases like proration and refunds are complex

## advantages
- clear state machines for subscription lifecycle
- strong audit trails improve support and compliance

## disadvantages
- edge cases like proration and refunds are complex
- external processor behavior and retries require careful idempotency

## anti_patterns
- using this when one-time purchases with no recurring charges
- using this when products delegating all billing and entitlements to a third-party with no internal logic
- ignoring the downside that edge cases like proration and refunds are complex

## real_world_examples
- upgrade/downgrade flows with proration and immediate entitlement changes
- dunning workflows with retries and account suspension rules
