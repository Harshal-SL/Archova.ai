# Social Media Feed

## chunk_id
- 12.8


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- a system that generates personalized timelines from posts, follows, reactions, and ranking signals at large scale.

## when_to_use
- planning fan-out, ranking, and caching strategies
- designing read models for personalized content delivery
- handling high write rates for posts and interactions

## when_not_to_use
- simple content sites without personalization or timelines
- small communities where naive queries can meet performance needs

## decision_signals
- need: planning fan-out, ranking, and caching strategies
- need: designing read models for personalized content delivery
- willing to accept: fan-out and ranking pipelines are complex to operate

## advantages
- supports separation of write path (posting) and read path (feed)
- allows specialized storage for timeline and ranking projections

## disadvantages
- fan-out and ranking pipelines are complex to operate
- consistency issues and late events can affect feed correctness

## anti_patterns
- using this when simple content sites without personalization or timelines
- using this when small communities where naive queries can meet performance needs
- ignoring the downside that fan-out and ranking pipelines are complex to operate

## real_world_examples
- fan-out on write to precompute timelines for followers
- hybrid feed combining precomputed items with real-time ranking
