# E-commerce Platform

## chunk_id
- 12.2


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- an online retail system that supports browsing, cart, checkout, payment, fulfillment, and post-purchase workflows.

## when_to_use
- modeling a reference architecture for transactional commerce
- planning scale and reliability for seasonal traffic spikes
- designing integrations for payments, inventory, and shipping

## when_not_to_use
- simple catalogs without checkout or fulfillment flows
- systems that do not require strong transactional guarantees

## decision_signals
- need: modeling a reference architecture for transactional commerce
- need: planning scale and reliability for seasonal traffic spikes
- willing to accept: complex consistency challenges across inventory and payments

## advantages
- clear bounded contexts (catalog, cart, orders, payments, fulfillment)
- supports modular scaling of read-heavy and write-heavy parts

## disadvantages
- complex consistency challenges across inventory and payments
- high risk of cascading failures during peak events

## anti_patterns
- using this when simple catalogs without checkout or fulfillment flows
- using this when systems that do not require strong transactional guarantees
- ignoring the downside that complex consistency challenges across inventory and payments

## real_world_examples
- flash sales requiring queueing, rate limits, and inventory reservation
- order status updates driven by events from fulfillment providers
