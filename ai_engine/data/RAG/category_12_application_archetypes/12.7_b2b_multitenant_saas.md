# B2B Multitenant SaaS

## chunk_id
- 12.7


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- a software-as-a-service product serving many organizations with tenant isolation, role management, billing, and configurable features.

## when_to_use
- shared infrastructure serving many customers
- needing tenant-scoped access controls and quotas
- offering per-tenant configuration and integrations

## when_not_to_use
- single-tenant deployments with dedicated infrastructure per customer
- consumer apps without organizational tenancy

## decision_signals
- need: shared infrastructure serving many customers
- need: needing tenant-scoped access controls and quotas
- willing to accept: tenant isolation mistakes are high impact

## advantages
- cost-efficient shared operations and scaling
- supports rapid onboarding of new customers

## disadvantages
- tenant isolation mistakes are high impact
- customization increases schema and performance complexity

## anti_patterns
- using this when single-tenant deployments with dedicated infrastructure per customer
- using this when consumer apps without organizational tenancy
- ignoring the downside that tenant isolation mistakes are high impact

## real_world_examples
- crm and helpdesk platforms
- multi-tenant analytics dashboards with per-tenant data models
