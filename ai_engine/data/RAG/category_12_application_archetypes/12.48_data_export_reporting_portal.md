# Data Export Reporting Portal

## chunk_id
- 12.48


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- an application that provides dashboards and exports over business data, often requiring heavy reads, joins, and scheduled report generation.

## when_to_use
- customers needing csv exports and scheduled reports
- internal analytics and compliance reporting
- separating reporting from core transactional workloads

## when_not_to_use
- small datasets where ad hoc queries are inexpensive
- systems where data cannot be exposed due to policy constraints

## decision_signals
- need: customers needing csv exports and scheduled reports
- need: internal analytics and compliance reporting
- willing to accept: expensive queries can degrade performance without isolation

## advantages
- improves user autonomy with self-serve reporting
- reduces load on primary services by using read replicas or warehouses

## disadvantages
- expensive queries can degrade performance without isolation
- exports can become a data exfiltration vector without controls

## anti_patterns
- using this when small datasets where ad hoc queries are inexpensive
- using this when systems where data cannot be exposed due to policy constraints
- ignoring the downside that expensive queries can degrade performance without isolation

## real_world_examples
- scheduled exports generated asynchronously and delivered via secure links
- dashboards backed by a warehouse and cached aggregates
