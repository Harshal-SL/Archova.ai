# Realtime Collaboration App

## chunk_id
- 12.24


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- an application where multiple users edit or interact with shared state simultaneously, requiring low-latency sync and conflict handling.

## when_to_use
- docs, whiteboards, shared design tools, and multiplayer editing
- needing presence, typing indicators, and instant updates
- supporting offline edits and later merging

## when_not_to_use
- single-user workflows with occasional sharing
- systems where eventual updates are acceptable without conflicts

## decision_signals
- need: docs, whiteboards, shared design tools, and multiplayer editing
- need: needing presence, typing indicators, and instant updates
- willing to accept: conflict resolution and ordering are complex

## advantages
- strong user experience through immediate feedback
- clear need for event streams and state reconciliation

## disadvantages
- conflict resolution and ordering are complex
- connection management and fan-out are costly at scale

## anti_patterns
- using this when single-user workflows with occasional sharing
- using this when systems where eventual updates are acceptable without conflicts
- ignoring the downside that conflict resolution and ordering are complex

## real_world_examples
- operational transform or crdt-based document editing
- presence service plus durable event log for changes
