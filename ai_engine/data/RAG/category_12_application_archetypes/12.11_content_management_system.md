# Content Management System

## chunk_id
- 12.11


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- an application for authoring, versioning, and publishing content with workflows, permissions, and multi-channel delivery.

## when_to_use
- teams creating pages, articles, media, and structured content
- needing draft/review/publish workflows and rollback
- delivering content to web, mobile, and external consumers

## when_not_to_use
- static sites with infrequent updates
- systems where content is purely user-generated with no editorial workflow

## decision_signals
- need: teams creating pages, articles, media, and structured content
- need: needing draft/review/publish workflows and rollback
- willing to accept: rich workflows can become complex and hard to customize

## advantages
- supports versioning, preview, and scheduled publishing
- centralizes content models and governance

## disadvantages
- rich workflows can become complex and hard to customize
- search, localization, and media pipelines add operational overhead

## anti_patterns
- using this when static sites with infrequent updates
- using this when systems where content is purely user-generated with no editorial workflow
- ignoring the downside that rich workflows can become complex and hard to customize

## real_world_examples
- marketing sites with campaign pages and approvals
- knowledge bases with article lifecycle and permissions
