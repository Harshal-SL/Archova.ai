# Observability Platform

## chunk_id
- 12.32


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- a system that collects logs, metrics, and traces, supports querying and alerting, and manages high-cardinality telemetry at scale.

## when_to_use
- building centralized monitoring for distributed systems
- supporting incident response and slos with alerting
- managing retention, sampling, and cost controls

## when_not_to_use
- small deployments where managed tooling fully covers needs
- environments without clear data retention and access policies

## decision_signals
- need: building centralized monitoring for distributed systems
- need: supporting incident response and slos with alerting
- willing to accept: high ingestion volume can be costly without sampling

## advantages
- enables faster debugging and reliability improvements
- supports correlation via trace_ids and structured events

## disadvantages
- high ingestion volume can be costly without sampling
- indexing and querying high-cardinality data is complex

## anti_patterns
- using this when small deployments where managed tooling fully covers needs
- using this when environments without clear data retention and access policies
- ignoring the downside that high ingestion volume can be costly without sampling

## real_world_examples
- tail-based trace sampling and metric aggregation pipelines
- log ingestion with schema normalization and tiered retention
