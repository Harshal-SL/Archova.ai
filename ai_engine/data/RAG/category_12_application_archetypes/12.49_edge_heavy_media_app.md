# Edge Heavy Media App

## chunk_id
- 12.49


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- an application that serves media globally with heavy use of cdn/edge caching and optimized delivery paths for low latency and high throughput.

## when_to_use
- video, audio, image, and large asset delivery
- needing low buffering, fast start, and global performance
- controlling bandwidth costs with cache efficiency

## when_not_to_use
- internal apps with small assets and limited distribution
- products where content is entirely dynamic and uncacheable

## decision_signals
- need: video, audio, image, and large asset delivery
- need: needing low buffering, fast start, and global performance
- willing to accept: cache invalidation and authorization at the edge can be complex

## advantages
- strong performance and scalability through edge caching
- reduces origin load and improves resilience to spikes

## disadvantages
- cache invalidation and authorization at the edge can be complex
- operational complexity for multi-region origins and signing strategies

## anti_patterns
- using this when internal apps with small assets and limited distribution
- using this when products where content is entirely dynamic and uncacheable
- ignoring the downside that cache invalidation and authorization at the edge can be complex

## real_world_examples
- immutable segment urls for adaptive streaming served from cdn
- signed urls or tokens validated at the edge for protected content
