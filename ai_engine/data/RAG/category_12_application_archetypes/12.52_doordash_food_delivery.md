# DoorDash Food Delivery

## chunk_id
- 12.52


## category
- application_archetypes

## type
- application_archetype

## definition
- a delivery marketplace coordinating customers, restaurants, and dashers with dispatch, routing, cancellations, and refunds.

## when_to_use
- modeling dispatch and order orchestration with real-time updates
- implementing cancellation/refund compensations across services
- handling peak demand with regional load isolation

## when_not_to_use
- simple shipping workflows without courier dispatch
- low-volume delivery where manual assignment works

## decision_signals
- dispatch decisions require low latency (<500ms) with geo partitioning
- order lifecycle includes many compensations (refunds, reassignments)
- high volume location events and status updates require streaming ingestion

## advantages
- saga-based order workflows handle partial failures well
- event-driven updates improve customer and restaurant experience

## disadvantages
- edge cases around cancellations are numerous and hard to test
- peak demand can overwhelm dispatch without rate limits/backpressure

## anti_patterns
- coupling dispatch to synchronous restaurant confirmation calls
- retrying courier assignment without jitter and caps
- mixing real-time tracking writes with transactional order updates in one store

## real_world_examples
- order lifecycle events driving notifications and courier reassignment
- dispatch partitioned by region with backpressure under load
