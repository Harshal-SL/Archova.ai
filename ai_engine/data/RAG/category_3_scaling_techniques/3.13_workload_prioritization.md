# Workload Prioritization

## chunk_id
- 3.13


## category
- scaling_techniques

## type
- scaling_technique

## definition
- allocating resources so higher-priority requests and jobs get served ahead of lower-priority work.

## when_to_use
- systems with critical user flows competing with background tasks
- preventing noisy neighbors in multi-tenant workloads
- enforcing business priority tiers and fairness policies

## when_not_to_use
- when work items cannot be safely reordered
- when priority signals are unclear and would be gamed or misused

## decision_signals
- need: systems with critical user flows competing with background tasks
- need: preventing noisy neighbors in multi-tenant workloads
- willing to accept: starvation risk for low-priority tasks without quotas

## advantages
- protects important flows under load
- improves predictability of latency for high-priority work

## disadvantages
- starvation risk for low-priority tasks without quotas
- increases complexity in scheduling and capacity planning

## anti_patterns
- use this when work items cannot be safely reordered
- use this when priority signals are unclear and would be gamed or misused
- ignoring the downside that starvation risk for low-priority tasks without quotas

## real_world_examples
- reserving database connections for checkout endpoints
- separate queues for realtime alerts vs batch processing
