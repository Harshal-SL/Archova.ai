# Slo Based Capacity Planning

## chunk_id
- 3.17


## category
- scaling_techniques

## type
- scaling_technique

## definition
- planning capacity using sli and slo targets so systems keep latency and error budgets under expected and peak load.

## when_to_use
- production systems with defined latency and availability slos
- preparing for known peaks like launches and seasonal events
- deciding headroom and autoscaling guardrails

## when_not_to_use
- early prototypes without clear user-facing objectives
- when measurement and instrumentation are missing or unreliable

## decision_signals
- need: production systems with defined latency and availability slos
- need: preparing for known peaks like launches and seasonal events
- willing to accept: requires accurate telemetry and realistic traffic models

## advantages
- aligns scaling decisions with user experience outcomes
- reduces over-provisioning by focusing on measurable targets

## disadvantages
- requires accurate telemetry and realistic traffic models
- can be undermined by poorly chosen slis or untracked dependencies

## anti_patterns
- using this when early prototypes without clear user-facing objectives
- use this when measurement and instrumentation are missing or unreliable
- ignoring the downside that requires accurate telemetry and realistic traffic models

## real_world_examples
- setting p95 latency slo and provisioning for peak plus headroom
- defining error budget policies that trigger scaling or shedding
