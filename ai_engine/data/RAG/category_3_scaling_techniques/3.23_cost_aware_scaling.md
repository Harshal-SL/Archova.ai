# Cost Aware Scaling

## chunk_id
- 3.23


## category
- scaling_techniques

## type
- scaling_technique

## definition
- scaling with cost constraints by using right-sizing, autoscaling guardrails, and workload classification to optimize spend.

## when_to_use
- workloads with predictable idle periods and bursty peaks
- multi-tenant platforms needing cost allocation and budgets
- services where over-provisioning is a major expense driver

## when_not_to_use
- systems where aggressive cost cuts would violate availability or latency slos
- when cost signals are unavailable or not tied to workloads and tenants

## decision_signals
- need: workloads with predictable idle periods and bursty peaks
- need: multi-tenant platforms needing cost allocation and budgets
- willing to accept: can create risk if guardrails are too strict during spikes

## advantages
- reduces waste while maintaining slos through targeted headroom
- improves decision making with cost attribution per workload

## disadvantages
- can create risk if guardrails are too strict during spikes
- requires ongoing monitoring and governance to prevent regressions

## anti_patterns
- using this when systems where aggressive cost cuts would violate availability or latency slos
- use this when cost signals are unavailable or not tied to workloads and tenants
- ignoring the downside that can create risk if guardrails are too strict during spikes

## real_world_examples
- separate autoscaling policies for critical vs best-effort services
- turning batch workers down during business hours to preserve latency budgets
