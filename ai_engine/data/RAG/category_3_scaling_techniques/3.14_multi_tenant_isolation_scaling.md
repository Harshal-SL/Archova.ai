# Multi Tenant Isolation Scaling

## chunk_id
- 3.14


## category
- scaling_techniques

## type
- scaling_technique

## definition
- scaling saas systems by isolating tenant workloads using quotas, partitioning, and per-tenant observability to control noisy neighbors.

## when_to_use
- shared-infrastructure saas with diverse tenant workloads
- protecting critical tenants or tiers with reserved capacity
- enforcing per-tenant limits for fairness and compliance

## when_not_to_use
- single-tenant systems or fully dedicated per-tenant deployments
- when per-tenant identifiers are missing from data and telemetry

## decision_signals
- need: shared-infrastructure saas with diverse tenant workloads
- need: protecting critical tenants or tiers with reserved capacity
- willing to accept: adds complexity to routing, data models, and reporting

## advantages
- improves stability by containing noisy tenants
- enables predictable scaling by measuring per-tenant consumption

## disadvantages
- adds complexity to routing, data models, and reporting
- incorrect limits can harm legitimate tenant traffic

## anti_patterns
- using this when single-tenant systems or fully dedicated per-tenant deployments
- use this when per-tenant identifiers are missing from data and telemetry
- ignoring the downside that adds complexity to routing, data models, and reporting

## real_world_examples
- per-tenant rate limits and connection pools at the gateway
- tenant-based sharding and hotspot detection
