# Autoscaling Policies

## chunk_id
- 3.4


## category
- scaling_techniques

## type
- scaling_technique

## definition
- automatically adjusting capacity based on signals like cpu, latency, queue depth, or custom metrics.

## when_to_use
- workloads with variable demand and predictable scaling signals
- services with safe horizontal scaling behavior
- queue-based systems where backlog is a strong scaling metric

## when_not_to_use
- stateful systems without safe scale out and scale in procedures
- when metrics are noisy and would cause thrashing without damping

## decision_signals
- need: workloads with variable demand and predictable scaling signals
- need: services with safe horizontal scaling behavior
- willing to accept: can thrash under noisy signals and cause instability

## advantages
- matches capacity to demand and reduces waste
- improves responsiveness to spikes and diurnal patterns

## disadvantages
- can thrash under noisy signals and cause instability
- cold start and warmup time can lag behind traffic spikes

## anti_patterns
- using this when stateful systems without safe scale out and scale in procedures
- use this when metrics are noisy and would cause thrashing without damping
- ignoring the downside that can thrash under noisy signals and cause instability

## real_world_examples
- scaling consumers based on queue depth and processing time
- scaling api replicas based on p95 latency and cpu
