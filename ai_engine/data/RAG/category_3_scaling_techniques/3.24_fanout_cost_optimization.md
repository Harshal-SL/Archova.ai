# Fanout Cost Optimization

## chunk_id
- 3.24


## category
- scaling_techniques

## type
- scaling_technique

## definition
- reducing compute, network, and storage cost of high fanout workloads by selective delivery, batching, and hierarchical distribution.

## when_to_use
- notification or feed systems with one-to-many delivery patterns
- workloads where fanout dominates infrastructure spend
- systems that require bounded latency under peak bursts

## when_not_to_use
- low-fanout systems with predictable small audiences
- strict per-recipient customization that prevents batching

## decision_signals
- need: notification or feed systems with one-to-many delivery patterns
- need: workloads where fanout dominates infrastructure spend
- willing to accept: additional complexity in delivery planning and caching

## advantages
- materially lowers infra cost at scale
- improves throughput by reducing redundant downstream work

## disadvantages
- additional complexity in delivery planning and caching
- tradeoffs between freshness, personalization, and efficiency

## anti_patterns
- broadcasting all events to all consumers regardless of relevance
- per-recipient synchronous fanout in request path
- ignoring per-tenant cost controls

## real_world_examples
- precomputing shard-level recipient lists and batched publish
- hybrid push/pull fanout for inactive users
