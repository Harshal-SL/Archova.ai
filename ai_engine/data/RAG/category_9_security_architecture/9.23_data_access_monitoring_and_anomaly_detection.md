# Data Access Monitoring and Anomaly Detection


## chunk_id
- 9.23

## category
- security_architecture

## type
- security_pattern

## definition
- detecting suspicious access by monitoring data reads, exports, and permission changes and alerting on anomalies.

## when_to_use
- systems with sensitive data and privileged admin actions
- detecting insider threats and compromised accounts
- meeting compliance requirements for access monitoring

## when_not_to_use
- when monitoring cannot be acted upon and alerts will be ignored
- when signals are too noisy and will generate constant false positives

## decision_signals
- need: systems with sensitive data and privileged admin actions
- need: detecting insider threats and compromised accounts
- willing to accept: anomaly models and rules require tuning and maintenance

## advantages
- improves early detection of exfiltration and abuse
- provides evidence for investigations and compliance audits

## disadvantages
- anomaly models and rules require tuning and maintenance
- high-cardinality events can be costly to store and analyze

## anti_patterns
- use this when monitoring cannot be acted upon and alerts will be ignored
- use this when signals are too noisy and will generate constant false positives
- ignoring the downside that anomaly models and rules require tuning and maintenance

## real_world_examples
- alerting on unusual bulk exports or access from new geographies
- monitoring spikes in authorization denials and admin role changes
