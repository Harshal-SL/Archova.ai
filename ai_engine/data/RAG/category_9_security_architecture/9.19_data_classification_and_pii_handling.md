# Data Classification and PII Handling


## chunk_id
- 9.19

## category
- security_architecture

## type
- security_pattern

## definition
- categorizing data by sensitivity and applying controls for collection, access, retention, and deletion of pii and secrets.

## when_to_use
- systems processing personal, financial, or health data
- multi-tenant platforms with shared storage and logging
- defining retention, deletion, and access controls for compliance

## when_not_to_use
- when classifications are undefined and enforcement cannot be implemented
- when data minimization would be ignored by product requirements

## decision_signals
- need: systems processing personal, financial, or health data
- need: multi-tenant platforms with shared storage and logging
- willing to accept: misclassification can cause either over-restriction or leaks

## advantages
- reduces privacy risk through minimization and controlled access
- supports compliance through clear retention and deletion rules

## disadvantages
- misclassification can cause either over-restriction or leaks
- enforcement across pipelines and logs requires disciplined implementation

## anti_patterns
- use this when classifications are undefined and enforcement cannot be implemented
- use this when data minimization would be ignored by product requirements
- ignoring the downside that misclassification can cause either over-restriction or leaks

## real_world_examples
- masking pii in logs and restricting access to raw data stores
- applying retention rules to delete or anonymize old customer records
