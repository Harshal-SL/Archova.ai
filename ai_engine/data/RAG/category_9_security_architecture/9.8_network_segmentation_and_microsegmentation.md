# Network Segmentation and Microsegmentation


## chunk_id
- 9.8

## category
- security_architecture

## type
- security_pattern

## definition
- limiting lateral movement by isolating networks and enforcing fine-grained traffic policies between services and tiers.

## when_to_use
- multi-tier architectures with different trust and exposure levels
- limiting blast radius of compromised workloads
- enforcing compliance boundaries between environments and tenants

## when_not_to_use
- when a flat network is sufficient and segmentation would cause operational fragility
- when policy management tooling is not available to avoid drift

## decision_signals
- need: multi-tier architectures with different trust and exposure levels
- need: limiting blast radius of compromised workloads
- willing to accept: misconfigured rules can cause outages and hard-to-debug failures

## advantages
- reduces attack surface and lateral movement opportunities
- improves clarity of allowed communication paths

## disadvantages
- misconfigured rules can cause outages and hard-to-debug failures
- policy sprawl can make changes slow and error-prone

## anti_patterns
- use this when a flat network is sufficient and segmentation would cause operational fragility
- use this when policy management tooling is not available to avoid drift
- ignoring the downside that misconfigured rules can cause outages and hard-to-debug failures

## real_world_examples
- isolating databases in private subnets with strict ingress rules
- allowing only specific service identities to call internal admin endpoints
