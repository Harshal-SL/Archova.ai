# Multi-Tenant Security Isolation


## chunk_id
- 9.20

## category
- security_architecture

## type
- security_pattern

## definition
- preventing cross-tenant data access and interference through identity scoping, key design, authorization, and resource controls.

## when_to_use
- any saas platform serving multiple customer organizations
- shared caches, brokers, and databases
- enforcing per-tenant quotas and operational boundaries

## when_not_to_use
- single-tenant deployments with dedicated infrastructure
- when tenant scoping cannot be made explicit in keys and policies

## decision_signals
- need: any saas platform serving multiple customer organizations
- need: shared caches, brokers, and databases
- willing to accept: mistakes are high impact and can be hard to detect

## advantages
- reduces risk of severe cross-tenant data leaks
- improves fairness and resilience by limiting noisy neighbors

## disadvantages
- mistakes are high impact and can be hard to detect
- increases complexity in key design, indexing, and policy evaluation

## anti_patterns
- using this when single-tenant deployments with dedicated infrastructure
- use this when tenant scoping cannot be made explicit in keys and policies
- ignoring the downside that mistakes are high impact and can be hard to detect

## real_world_examples
- prefixing primary keys, indexes, and cache keys with tenant_id
- enforcing tenant-scoped access checks at gateway and service layers
