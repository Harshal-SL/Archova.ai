# mTLS and Service Identity


## chunk_id
- 9.22

## category
- security_architecture

## type
- security_pattern

## definition
- authenticating services to each other using mutual tls and workload identities so internal traffic is encrypted and authorized.

## when_to_use
- microservices with east-west traffic over shared networks
- zero trust adoption for internal service calls
- preventing impersonation and man-in-the-middle on internal links

## when_not_to_use
- small systems where operational overhead outweighs benefit
- environments without reliable certificate issuance and rotation

## decision_signals
- need: microservices with east-west traffic over shared networks
- need: zero trust adoption for internal service calls
- willing to accept: certificate management and rotation add complexity

## advantages
- provides strong service authentication and encryption in transit
- enables fine-grained authorization based on service identity

## disadvantages
- certificate management and rotation add complexity
- misconfiguration can cause widespread communication failures

## anti_patterns
- using this when small systems where operational overhead outweighs benefit
- using this when environments without reliable certificate issuance and rotation
- ignoring the downside that certificate management and rotation add complexity

## real_world_examples
- enforcing mTLS between services via a service mesh
- issuing short-lived certificates bound to workload identity
