# Incident Response and Playbooks


## chunk_id
- 9.18

## category
- security_architecture

## type
- security_pattern

## definition
- preparing processes, roles, and runbooks to detect, contain, eradicate, and recover from security incidents.

## when_to_use
- any production system with external exposure
- reducing time to respond to credential compromise and data leakage
- meeting compliance requirements for incident handling

## when_not_to_use
- when playbooks are untested and not integrated with on-call workflows
- when detection and logging are insufficient to support response

## decision_signals
- need: any production system with external exposure
- need: reducing time to respond to credential compromise and data leakage
- willing to accept: requires ongoing drills and maintenance as systems change

## advantages
- reduces mean time to contain and recover
- improves coordination and decision making under pressure

## disadvantages
- requires ongoing drills and maintenance as systems change
- over-reliance on playbooks can miss novel attacks without judgment

## anti_patterns
- use this when playbooks are untested and not integrated with on-call workflows
- use this when detection and logging are insufficient to support response
- ignoring the downside that requires ongoing drills and maintenance as systems change

## real_world_examples
- a playbook for key compromise that triggers rotation and token revocation
- incident drills that validate audit logs and access review workflows
