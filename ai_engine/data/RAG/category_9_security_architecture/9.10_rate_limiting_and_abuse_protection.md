# Rate Limiting and Abuse Protection


## chunk_id
- 9.10

## category
- security_architecture

## type
- security_pattern

## definition
- limiting request rates and enforcing quotas to protect services from abuse, bots, and accidental overload.

## when_to_use
- public endpoints exposed to the internet
- multi-tenant platforms requiring fairness and plan enforcement
- protecting expensive operations and downstream dependencies

## when_not_to_use
- when limits would break critical emergency workflows without bypass rules
- internal-only systems with negligible abuse risk

## decision_signals
- need: public endpoints exposed to the internet
- need: multi-tenant platforms requiring fairness and plan enforcement
- willing to accept: incorrect limits cause widespread client failures

## advantages
- improves availability by preventing resource exhaustion
- supports predictable tenant fairness and billing controls

## disadvantages
- incorrect limits cause widespread client failures
- distributed enforcement can be complex and costly

## anti_patterns
- use this when limits would break critical emergency workflows without bypass rules
- using this when internal-only systems with negligible abuse risk
- ignoring the downside that incorrect limits cause widespread client failures

## real_world_examples
- applying per-api-key limits with clear retry-after responses
- adding bot protection and rate-based blocks at the edge
