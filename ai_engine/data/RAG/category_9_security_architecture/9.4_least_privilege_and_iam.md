# Least Privilege and IAM


## chunk_id
- 9.4

## category
- security_architecture

## type
- security_pattern

## definition
- granting identities the minimum permissions needed for their tasks and continuously reviewing and narrowing access.

## when_to_use
- any system with service accounts, humans, and automation
- reducing blast radius of compromised credentials
- meeting compliance requirements for access governance

## when_not_to_use
- emergency break-glass scenarios without time for least-privilege tuning (add follow-up cleanup)
- environments that cannot audit permissions or track ownership

## decision_signals
- need: any system with service accounts, humans, and automation
- need: reducing blast radius of compromised credentials
- willing to accept: requires ongoing maintenance as systems evolve

## advantages
- reduces damage from accidental or malicious actions
- improves auditability and access reviews

## disadvantages
- requires ongoing maintenance as systems evolve
- overly strict permissions can cause outages and deployment failures

## anti_patterns
- using this when emergency break-glass scenarios without time for least-privilege tuning (add follow-up cleanup)
- using this when environments that cannot audit permissions or track ownership
- ignoring the downside that requires ongoing maintenance as systems evolve

## real_world_examples
- limiting a worker role to read-only access for specific buckets and topics
- using separate identities per service and per environment
