# Backup Security and Ransomware Resilience


## chunk_id
- 9.24

## category
- security_architecture

## type
- security_pattern

## definition
- protecting backups with isolation, immutability, and access controls so recovery is possible even during credential compromise.

## when_to_use
- protecting critical data from ransomware and accidental deletes
- meeting business continuity requirements
- defending against privileged account compromise

## when_not_to_use
- when backups are untested and restore procedures are unknown
- when backup access is shared broadly without least privilege

## decision_signals
- need: protecting critical data from ransomware and accidental deletes
- need: meeting business continuity requirements
- willing to accept: adds cost for redundant and immutable storage

## advantages
- increases likelihood of recovery after destructive incidents
- reduces attacker ability to delete or corrupt backups

## disadvantages
- adds cost for redundant and immutable storage
- requires regular restore drills and access governance

## anti_patterns
- use this when backups are untested and restore procedures are unknown
- use this when backup access is shared broadly without least privilege
- ignoring the downside that adds cost for redundant and immutable storage

## real_world_examples
- immutable backup snapshots with separate credentials and restricted access
- regular restore tests into isolated environments with application validation
