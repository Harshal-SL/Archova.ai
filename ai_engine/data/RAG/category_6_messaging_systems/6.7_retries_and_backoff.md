# Retries And Backoff

## chunk_id
- 6.7


## category
- messaging_systems

## type
- messaging_pattern

## definition
- re-attempting failed message processing with delay strategies to reduce contention and avoid thundering herds.

## when_to_use
- transient errors like timeouts and rate limits
- dependent services that recover quickly
- smoothing consumer load during partial outages

## when_not_to_use
- deterministic failures that will never succeed without human action
- when retries would amplify load on a struggling dependency

## decision_signals
- need: transient errors like timeouts and rate limits
- need: dependent services that recover quickly
- willing to accept: can increase end-to-end latency and backlog growth

## advantages
- improves success rates without manual intervention
- reduces immediate pressure on downstream systems

## disadvantages
- can increase end-to-end latency and backlog growth
- unbounded retries can cause infinite poison message loops

## anti_patterns
- using this when deterministic failures that will never succeed without human action
- use this when retries would amplify load on a struggling dependency
- ignoring the downside that can increase end-to-end latency and backlog growth

## real_world_examples
- exponential backoff for temporary database lock timeouts
- limiting retries and routing to a dead letter queue after n attempts
