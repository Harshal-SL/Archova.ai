# Multi Region Messaging

## chunk_id
- 6.23


## category
- messaging_systems

## type
- messaging_pattern

## definition
- deploying messaging infrastructure across regions to improve latency and availability, while managing replication and failover.

## when_to_use
- consumers and producers are globally distributed
- regional outages must not stop critical event flows
- meeting data residency and locality requirements

## when_not_to_use
- when a single region meets latency and availability slos
- when conflict resolution and replication semantics are not well understood

## decision_signals
- need: consumers and producers are globally distributed
- need: regional outages must not stop critical event flows
- willing to accept: increases complexity for replication, ordering, and id generation

## advantages
- improves resilience to regional failures
- reduces latency by keeping traffic closer to users and services

## disadvantages
- increases complexity for replication, ordering, and id generation
- can create split-brain and divergence during partitions

## anti_patterns
- use this when a single region meets latency and availability slos
- use this when conflict resolution and replication semantics are not well understood
- ignoring the downside that increases complexity for replication, ordering, and id generation

## real_world_examples
- active-passive broker clusters with automated failover
- regional topics replicated with defined lag and cutover procedures
