# Consumer Lag Handling Strategies

## chunk_id
- 6.26


## category
- messaging_systems

## type
- messaging_pattern

## definition
- techniques for detecting, controlling, and recovering from consumer lag without destabilizing brokers or downstream systems.

## when_to_use
- stream consumers that fall behind during peaks or incidents
- pipelines with strict freshness slos
- systems requiring graceful degradation under backlog growth

## when_not_to_use
- tiny queues with negligible backlog risk
- offline batch systems where lag is expected and acceptable

## decision_signals
- need: stream consumers that fall behind during peaks or incidents
- need: pipelines with strict freshness slos
- willing to accept: tradeoffs between data freshness and processing completeness

## advantages
- improves recovery predictability during spikes
- prevents runaway backlog from causing cascading failures

## disadvantages
- tradeoffs between data freshness and processing completeness
- aggressive catch-up can overload dependencies

## anti_patterns
- scaling consumers blindly without checking downstream limits
- replaying backlog at full speed after outage recovery
- lacking lag-based alert thresholds and runbooks

## real_world_examples
- lag-aware autoscaling with max concurrency caps
- dropping low-priority events when lag breaches critical thresholds
