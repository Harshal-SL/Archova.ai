# Event Ordering Vs Throughput Tradeoff

## chunk_id
- 6.25


## category
- messaging_systems

## type
- messaging_pattern

## definition
- balancing strict event order guarantees against partition parallelism and throughput in queue and stream processing systems.

## when_to_use
- designing partition keys and consumer concurrency models
- workflows where some entities require in-order updates
- systems hitting throughput ceilings due to ordering constraints

## when_not_to_use
- workloads where event order has no business impact
- pipelines with very low volume and no scaling pressure

## decision_signals
- need: workflows where some entities require in-order updates
- need: systems hitting throughput ceilings due to ordering constraints
- willing to accept: per-key serialization and potential hotspot partitions

## advantages
- clarifies where strict ordering is truly needed
- enables targeted throughput gains with scoped ordering

## disadvantages
- per-key serialization and potential hotspot partitions
- reordering and replay logic can become complex

## anti_patterns
- enforcing global order for unrelated entities
- selecting partition keys without cardinality analysis
- conflating broker order with end-to-end business order

## real_world_examples
- ordering by account_id while processing accounts in parallel
- using sequence numbers and conflict checks in consumers
