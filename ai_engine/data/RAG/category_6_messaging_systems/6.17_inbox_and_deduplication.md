# Inbox And Deduplication

## chunk_id
- 6.17


## category
- messaging_systems

## type
- messaging_pattern

## definition
- storing receipt and processing markers for messages so retries and redelivery do not cause duplicate side effects.

## when_to_use
- at-least-once delivery with consumer restarts
- handlers that perform non-idempotent external calls
- ensuring exactly-once effects at the application level

## when_not_to_use
- when messages are guaranteed once and side effects are naturally idempotent
- when storing dedupe state is more expensive than tolerating duplicates

## decision_signals
- need: at-least-once delivery with consumer restarts
- need: handlers that perform non-idempotent external calls
- willing to accept: adds storage, indexing, and cleanup requirements

## advantages
- enables safe reprocessing and resilience to crash recovery
- provides auditable evidence of processed messages

## disadvantages
- adds storage, indexing, and cleanup requirements
- can reduce throughput if the inbox becomes a hotspot

## anti_patterns
- use this when messages are guaranteed once and side effects are naturally idempotent
- use this when storing dedupe state is more expensive than tolerating duplicates
- ignoring the downside that adds storage, indexing, and cleanup requirements

## real_world_examples
- recording processed message ids per consumer group with a ttl
- using a unique constraint on (consumer, message_id) to enforce dedupe
