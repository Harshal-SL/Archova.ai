# Security And Authorization In Messaging

## chunk_id
- 6.22


## category
- messaging_systems

## type
- messaging_pattern

## definition
- protecting brokers and topics using authentication, authorization, encryption, and tenant isolation.

## when_to_use
- multi-tenant or multi-team brokers shared across applications
- events containing sensitive data or business-critical operations
- enforcing least privilege on publish and consume permissions

## when_not_to_use
- when messaging is local-only and contains no sensitive data (still keep basic auth)
- when a broker cannot enforce topic-level permissions and isolation is required

## decision_signals
- need: multi-tenant or multi-team brokers shared across applications
- need: events containing sensitive data or business-critical operations
- willing to accept: adds operational complexity for certificates, keys, and rbac

## advantages
- reduces risk of data leaks and unauthorized actions
- improves compliance with auditable access controls

## disadvantages
- adds operational complexity for certificates, keys, and rbac
- misconfiguration can cause outages or silent drops

## anti_patterns
- use this when messaging is local-only and contains no sensitive data (still keep basic auth)
- use this when a broker cannot enforce topic-level permissions and isolation is required
- ignoring the downside that adds operational complexity for certificates, keys, and rbac

## real_world_examples
- restricting who can publish to payment_events while allowing read-only consumers
- encrypting traffic in transit and rotating credentials periodically
