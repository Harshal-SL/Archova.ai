# Operational Monitoring For Messaging

## chunk_id
- 6.24


## category
- messaging_systems

## type
- messaging_pattern

## definition
- observing broker and consumer health using lag, throughput, error rates, and saturation signals to prevent outages.

## when_to_use
- running production brokers and consumer fleets
- detecting backlog growth, stuck partitions, and poison storms
- enforcing slos for event-driven pipelines

## when_not_to_use
- when messaging is dev-only and not operationally critical
- when monitoring cannot be acted upon (no on-call or automation)

## decision_signals
- need: running production brokers and consumer fleets
- need: detecting backlog growth, stuck partitions, and poison storms
- willing to accept: noisy alerts can cause alert fatigue if poorly tuned

## advantages
- detects problems early before backlogs become outages
- supports capacity planning and safe scaling decisions

## disadvantages
- noisy alerts can cause alert fatigue if poorly tuned
- metrics are broker-specific and require careful interpretation

## anti_patterns
- use this when messaging is dev-only and not operationally critical
- use this when monitoring cannot be acted upon (no on-call or automation)
- ignoring the downside that noisy alerts can cause alert fatigue if poorly tuned

## real_world_examples
- alerting on consumer lag rate-of-change instead of absolute lag alone
- monitoring publish/consume error rates and retry counts per topic
