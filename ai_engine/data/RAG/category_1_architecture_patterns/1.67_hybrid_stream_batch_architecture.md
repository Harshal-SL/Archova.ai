# Hybrid Stream-Batch Architecture


## chunk_id
- 1.67

## category
- architecture_patterns

## type
- architecture

## definition
- an architecture that combines streaming and batch processing to balance low-latency updates with accurate backfills and recomputation.

## when_to_use
- realtime dashboards that also need periodic reconciliation
- streaming pipelines that require accurate historical recomputation
- systems migrating from batch to streaming incrementally

## when_not_to_use
- teams that cannot support two processing paths and data models
- when a single approach like kappa or batch is sufficient

## decision_signals
- need: realtime dashboards that also need periodic reconciliation
- need: streaming pipelines that require accurate historical recomputation
- willing to accept: higher complexity and risk of duplicated business logic

## advantages
- delivers low-latency results while retaining batch correctness options
- supports backfills and data quality fixes without full rebuilds

## disadvantages
- higher complexity and risk of duplicated business logic
- requires careful consistency rules between stream and batch outputs

## anti_patterns
- using this when teams that cannot support two processing paths and data models
- use this when a single approach like kappa or batch is sufficient
- ignoring the downside that higher complexity and risk of duplicated business logic

## real_world_examples
- streaming metrics with nightly batch correction job
- fraud signals streaming with periodic full-history recomputation
