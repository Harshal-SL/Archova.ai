# Monolithic Architecture


## chunk_id
- 1.1

## category
- architecture_patterns

## type
- architecture

## definition
- A software architecture where the entire application is built, deployed, and executed as a single unified system.

## when_to_use
- small to medium applications
- early stage startups or MVPs
- simple business logic systems
- small development teams

## when_not_to_use
- large scale systems with high traffic
- systems needing independent scaling
- applications with multiple teams working independently

## decision_signals
- need: small to medium applications
- need: early stage startups or MVPs
- willing to accept: difficult to scale specific components

## advantages
- simple development and deployment
- easy debugging and testing
- low operational complexity
- faster initial development

## disadvantages
- difficult to scale specific components
- tight coupling between modules
- full redeployment required for small changes
- large codebase becomes hard to maintain

## anti_patterns
- using this when large scale systems with high traffic
- using this when systems needing independent scaling
- ignoring the downside that difficult to scale specific components

## real_world_examples
- early Instagram
- early Facebook
