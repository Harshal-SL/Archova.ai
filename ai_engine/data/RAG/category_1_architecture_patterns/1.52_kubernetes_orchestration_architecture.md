# Kubernetes Orchestration Architecture


## chunk_id
- 1.52

## category
- architecture_patterns

## type
- architecture

## definition
- an architecture approach that uses kubernetes to schedule, scale, and manage containerized workloads across a cluster.

## when_to_use
- many containerized services needing autoscaling and self-healing
- standardized deployment primitives across teams and environments
- workloads needing advanced networking, service discovery, and policy

## when_not_to_use
- small teams without capacity to operate cluster complexity
- simple apps better served by managed serverless or paas platforms

## decision_signals
- need: many containerized services needing autoscaling and self-healing
- need: standardized deployment primitives across teams and environments
- willing to accept: steep operational learning curve and ongoing maintenance

## advantages
- provides robust scheduling, rollout, and recovery capabilities
- supports standardized observability and policy enforcement

## disadvantages
- steep operational learning curve and ongoing maintenance
- misconfiguration risk across networking, security, and storage

## anti_patterns
- using this when small teams without capacity to operate cluster complexity
- using this when simple apps better served by managed serverless or paas platforms
- ignoring the downside that steep operational learning curve and ongoing maintenance

## real_world_examples
- platform teams running multi-service applications on kubernetes
- data processing jobs running as kubernetes workloads
