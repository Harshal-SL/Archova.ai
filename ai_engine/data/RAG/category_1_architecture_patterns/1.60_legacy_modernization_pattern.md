# Legacy Modernization Pattern


## chunk_id
- 1.60

## category
- architecture_patterns

## type
- pattern

## definition
- a set of approaches for evolving legacy systems through refactoring, replatforming, and incremental replacement while controlling risk.

## when_to_use
- legacy systems blocking delivery speed or reliability
- reducing operational risk by improving observability and deployment practices
- migrating off end-of-life platforms and dependencies

## when_not_to_use
- when a system is stable, low change, and cost of change outweighs benefits
- when modernization goals and success metrics are not defined

## decision_signals
- need: legacy systems blocking delivery speed or reliability
- need: reducing operational risk by improving observability and deployment practices
- willing to accept: can take long and compete with product feature work

## advantages
- improves maintainability, security, and delivery velocity over time
- avoids big bang rewrites by sequencing improvements

## disadvantages
- can take long and compete with product feature work
- risk of partial modernization that increases complexity temporarily

## anti_patterns
- use this when a system is stable, low change, and cost of change outweighs benefits
- use this when modernization goals and success metrics are not defined
- ignoring the downside that can take long and compete with product feature work

## real_world_examples
- lifting a monolith into containers then extracting services gradually
- replacing a legacy message bus with event streaming over time
