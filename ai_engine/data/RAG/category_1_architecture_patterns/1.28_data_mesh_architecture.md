# Data Mesh Architecture


## chunk_id
- 1.28

## category
- architecture_patterns

## type
- architecture

## definition
- a data architecture where domains own data products and a platform provides shared self-serve capabilities and governance.

## when_to_use
- large organizations with many domain teams and data producers
- data platform scaling issues with a centralized data team
- high data reuse needs across multiple business domains

## when_not_to_use
- small organizations with limited domains and low data volume
- teams without strong data governance and ownership culture

## decision_signals
- need: large organizations with many domain teams and data producers
- need: data platform scaling issues with a centralized data team
- willing to accept: requires significant governance, standards, and platform investment

## advantages
- improves domain ownership and data product quality
- scales data development through federated responsibilities

## disadvantages
- requires significant governance, standards, and platform investment
- can fragment data if product contracts are not enforced

## anti_patterns
- using this when small organizations with limited domains and low data volume
- using this when teams without strong data governance and ownership culture
- ignoring the downside that requires significant governance, standards, and platform investment

## real_world_examples
- large enterprises adopting domain-owned data products
- organizations using lakehouse platforms with federated governance
