# Robinhood Trading Platform

## chunk_id
- 10.16


## category
- real_world_systems

## type
- real_world_system

## definition
- a retail trading system that accepts orders, interfaces with markets, updates positions, and enforces strong risk controls and auditing.

## when_to_use
- modeling order state machines, risk checks, and execution reporting
- designing low-latency decision paths with strict correctness
- handling market spikes with throttling and degraded modes

## when_not_to_use
- non-financial systems without regulated audit needs
- apps where correctness and risk controls are not first-class

## decision_signals
- strict auditability and immutable order history required
- tail latency and availability targets under market spikes
- external market/exchange dependencies require robust retry + failover

## advantages
- clear invariants drive strong state machines and auditing
- risk controls can be centralized and consistently enforced

## disadvantages
- external dependencies introduce complex failure handling
- correctness constraints limit naive scaling approaches

## anti_patterns
- accepting orders without idempotency and deduplication
- updating balances with non-atomic operations and no ledger
- retrying executions without clear idempotent identifiers

## real_world_examples
- order lifecycle events feeding positions and notifications
- throttling new orders during market volatility to protect stability
