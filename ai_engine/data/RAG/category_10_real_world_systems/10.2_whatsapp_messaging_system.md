# WhatsApp Messaging System

## chunk_id
- 10.2


## category
- real_world_systems

## type
- real_world_system

## definition
- a chat system optimized for reliable delivery, offline sync, and massive concurrent connections with end-to-end encryption constraints.

## when_to_use
- designing messaging with offline delivery, multi-device sync, and durable history
- supporting high fan-out with connection management and backpressure
- enforcing privacy constraints that limit server-side content inspection

## when_not_to_use
- simple notification delivery where ordering and history are not required
- low-concurrency environments without persistent connections

## decision_signals
- concurrent connections per region (>100k) with websocket-like channels
- ordering per conversation required with at-least-once delivery
- offline delivery and device re-sync are core requirements

## advantages
- partitions naturally by conversation_id for ordering and storage
- robust retry and dedup patterns for intermittent connectivity

## disadvantages
- presence and fan-out are expensive without careful state design
- end-to-end encryption limits server-side search and moderation

## anti_patterns
- relying on exactly-once delivery guarantees instead of idempotency
- storing presence state without expiry leading to unbounded growth
- making message send synchronous on all fan-out recipients

## real_world_examples
- durable queues for offline recipients with retry and expiry
- per-thread message ordering and read receipts
