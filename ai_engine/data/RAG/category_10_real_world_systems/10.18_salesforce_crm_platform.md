# Salesforce Crm Platform

## chunk_id
- 10.18


## category
- real_world_systems

## type
- real_world_system

## definition
- a multi-tenant enterprise crm platform with strong authorization, customization, reporting, and integration capabilities.

## when_to_use
- modeling multi-tenant business data with configurable schemas and workflows
- designing permission-heavy enterprise apps with audit requirements
- supporting integrations, exports, and reporting at scale

## when_not_to_use
- single-tenant apps with minimal customization needs
- systems without strict access control and audit requirements

## decision_signals
- tenant isolation and role-based permissions are critical
- customization requires metadata-driven configuration and extension points
- reporting/export workloads need isolation from transactional writes

## advantages
- metadata-driven design enables per-tenant customization safely
- strong audit and permission models support enterprise governance

## disadvantages
- customization increases complexity in indexing and performance
- reporting and exports can be expensive without dedicated read models

## anti_patterns
- mixing per-tenant custom fields into core tables without governance
- allowing exports without throttling and auditing
- using shared caches without tenant-aware keying

## real_world_examples
- per-tenant workflows and approval rules modeled in metadata
- audit logs for admin actions and permission changes
