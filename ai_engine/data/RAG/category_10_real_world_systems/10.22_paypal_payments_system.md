# PayPal Payments System

## chunk_id
- 10.22


## category
- real_world_systems

## type
- real_world_system

## definition
- a payments and wallet system that processes transfers and merchant payments with strong security, fraud controls, and reconciliation.

## when_to_use
- modeling wallet balances, transfers, and idempotent transaction processing
- designing fraud detection and account protection workflows
- supporting dispute handling with auditability

## when_not_to_use
- products that only need a third-party hosted checkout with no wallet logic
- domains without financial correctness and compliance requirements

## decision_signals
- append-only ledger required with immutable transaction ids
- fraud/risk scoring required for high-value or high-volume transfers
- strong security controls for keys, sessions, and audits

## advantages
- clear invariants and audit needs improve correctness and trust
- separation of risk scoring supports iterative fraud defenses

## disadvantages
- external dependencies and compliance requirements add complexity
- failures can be high impact and require careful rollback/compensation

## anti_patterns
- updating balances without a ledger and reconciliation
- processing transfers without idempotency keys
- logging sensitive payment data without redaction and access controls

## real_world_examples
- wallet ledger with reconciliation to bank settlement reports
- risk scoring on transfers with manual review for anomalies
