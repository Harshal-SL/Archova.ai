# Netflix Streaming Platform

## chunk_id
- 10.1


## category
- real_world_systems

## type
- real_world_system

## definition
- a global video streaming system focused on low buffering, personalized discovery, and resilient delivery under massive read traffic.

## when_to_use
- designing media delivery with global audiences and high cache hit targets
- building personalization pipelines that combine batch + real-time signals
- supporting peak traffic spikes with graceful degradation

## when_not_to_use
- small audiences where cdn + object storage is sufficient without complex personalization
- strict real-time consistency requirements across regions for user state

## decision_signals
- read-heavy workload (>95% reads) with global distribution
- latency targets for playback start (<2s) and low rebuffer rates
- multi-region delivery with cdn offload as primary scale lever

## advantages
- separates ingest/transcode from delivery and personalization paths
- strong resilience patterns for dependency failures during peaks

## disadvantages
- high operational complexity across edge, playback, and recommendation systems
- cost sensitivity to bandwidth, storage, and multi-bitrate encodes

## anti_patterns
- coupling playback delivery to synchronous personalization dependencies
- storing large media binaries in primary databases
- using unbounded retries for playback metadata calls under outage

## real_world_examples
- adaptive bitrate (hls/dash) segments served via cdn with immutable urls
- personalized home rows generated from events + offline models
