# Amazon E-commerce System

## chunk_id
- 10.3


## category
- real_world_systems

## type
- real_world_system

## definition
- a large-scale commerce system handling catalog discovery, cart/checkout, payments, and fulfillment with strong availability and peak-event resilience.

## when_to_use
- modeling transactional workflows with inventory, payments, and fulfillment integration
- designing for extreme seasonal spikes and flash-sale dynamics
- separating read-heavy catalog/search from write-heavy orders

## when_not_to_use
- small single-vendor shops where a hosted commerce platform suffices
- systems without complex fulfillment and post-purchase workflows

## decision_signals
- read traffic >> write traffic (catalog browsing dominates)
- checkout correctness requirements with idempotent payment flows
- peak events require queueing and rate limiting to protect order services

## advantages
- clear bounded contexts reduce coupling (catalog, cart, orders, fulfillment)
- event-driven workflows support asynchronous fulfillment updates

## disadvantages
- complex consistency and reservation logic for inventory
- high risk of cascading failures during peak traffic without isolation

## anti_patterns
- coupling checkout to synchronous fulfillment provider calls
- using a single shared database for all domains at high scale
- retrying payment capture without idempotency keys

## real_world_examples
- inventory holds with expiration and compensating releases
- order events driving shipping updates and customer notifications
