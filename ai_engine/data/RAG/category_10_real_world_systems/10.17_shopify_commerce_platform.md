# Shopify Commerce Platform

## chunk_id
- 10.17


## category
- real_world_systems

## type
- real_world_system

## definition
- a multi-tenant commerce platform that provides storefronts, checkout, payments, and app integrations for many merchants.

## when_to_use
- modeling multi-tenant commerce with extensibility and integrations
- designing merchant-specific themes, apps, and configuration
- supporting high traffic storefronts with strong caching and cdn

## when_not_to_use
- single-merchant platforms without tenant customization
- systems where extensibility and app ecosystems are unnecessary

## decision_signals
- multi-tenant isolation required with merchant-scoped data and quotas
- plugin/app model needs stable extension points and governance
- traffic spikes on merchant stores require edge caching and isolation

## advantages
- extensible architecture supports ecosystem growth
- shared platform operations reduce per-merchant overhead

## disadvantages
- tenant customization increases schema and performance complexity
- extension security and quality control are ongoing challenges

## anti_patterns
- allowing extensions to access data without strict tenant scoping
- coupling core checkout to third-party app availability
- sharing caches across tenants without namespacing

## real_world_examples
- app marketplace with webhooks and permissions
- storefront caching and cdn delivery for static assets
