# Wikipedia Content Platform

## chunk_id
- 10.21


## category
- real_world_systems

## type
- real_world_system

## definition
- a collaborative content system with revision history, caching, global reads, and editorial workflows for community moderation.

## when_to_use
- modeling versioned content with audit trails and rollback
- designing cache-heavy delivery for mostly read traffic
- supporting moderation and governance workflows

## when_not_to_use
- systems without collaborative editing or revision history
- workloads requiring strict real-time consistency for all readers

## decision_signals
- read-heavy traffic (>95%) with a smaller write/editor population
- revision history required with rollback and diffs
- cache strategy required for hot pages and global distribution

## advantages
- versioned content model supports audits and rollback
- caching delivers performance at large read scale

## disadvantages
- vandalism and abuse require moderation tooling
- cache invalidation can be tricky for highly edited pages

## anti_patterns
- updating pages without preserving revision history
- caching without invalidation and purge workflows
- mixing moderation actions without audit logs and attribution

## real_world_examples
- revision history with diffs and page rollback
- cdn caching for hot articles with purges on edits
