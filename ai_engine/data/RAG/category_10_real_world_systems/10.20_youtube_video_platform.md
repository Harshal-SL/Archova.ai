# YouTube Video Platform

## chunk_id
- 10.20


## category
- real_world_systems

## type
- real_world_system

## definition
- a video platform that ingests uploads, transcodes, stores, and serves video globally with search, recommendations, and content moderation.

## when_to_use
- modeling large media ingestion and asynchronous processing pipelines
- designing global playback with cdn caching and adaptive bitrate
- integrating moderation, metadata, and recommendation systems

## when_not_to_use
- small video libraries without global distribution needs
- systems where recommendations and search are not core

## decision_signals
- upload volume requires async transcoding with dlqs for failures
- playback requires cdn offload and multi-bitrate encodes
- discovery relies on search + recommendation ranking under latency budgets

## advantages
- strong pipeline separation improves reliability and scaling
- cdn delivery reduces buffering and origin load

## disadvantages
- high compute and storage cost for transcoding and retention
- moderation and policy enforcement are complex and continuous

## anti_patterns
- running transcoding synchronously on upload requests
- serving large segments directly from origin without caching strategy
- ignoring moderation signals in recommendation ranking inputs

## real_world_examples
- transcoding workers producing multiple renditions and thumbnails
- search index projection for video metadata and tags
