# Spotify Recommendation Engine

## chunk_id
- 10.25


## category
- real_world_systems

## type
- real_world_system

## definition
- a multi-stage recommendation architecture combining retrieval, ranking, and personalization with online feedback loops and strict latency targets.

## when_to_use
- content platforms serving highly personalized feeds or playlists
- systems balancing relevance, diversity, and freshness
- products with strong experimentation and model iteration needs

## when_not_to_use
- small catalogs with limited personalization benefits
- systems lacking event tracking for user feedback signals

## decision_signals
- need: content platforms serving highly personalized feeds or playlists
- need: products with strong experimentation and model iteration needs
- willing to accept: model serving complexity and feature freshness risk

## advantages
- improves engagement through layered ranking and personalization
- supports rapid experimentation with retrieval and ranker variants

## disadvantages
- model serving complexity and feature freshness risk
- feedback loops can over-optimize short-term engagement

## anti_patterns
- using a single global popularity list as the primary recommender
- skipping exploration controls and diversity constraints
- training and serving on inconsistent feature definitions

## real_world_examples
- candidate retrieval from embeddings followed by context-aware ranking
- blending editorial, collaborative, and recent activity signals
