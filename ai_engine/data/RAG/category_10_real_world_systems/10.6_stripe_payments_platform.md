# Stripe Payments Platform

## chunk_id
- 10.6


## category
- real_world_systems

## type
- real_world_system

## definition
- a payments platform that executes authorization/capture/refund flows with strict idempotency, audit trails, and reconciliation.

## when_to_use
- designing money movement workflows with external processor dependencies
- modeling ledgers, dispute handling, and compliance-driven auditing
- building retry-safe pipelines for at-least-once events

## when_not_to_use
- products using hosted checkout only with no internal payment logic
- systems where financial correctness and auditability are not required

## decision_signals
- idempotency required for all write operations (capture/refund)
- reconciliation needed against external reports (daily or hourly)
- security controls for secrets and keys are mandatory (rotation + audit)

## advantages
- append-only audit logs support investigations and compliance
- robust idempotency reduces duplicate charges under retries

## disadvantages
- high operational burden for compliance and security controls
- failure handling is complex due to external dependencies

## anti_patterns
- issuing refunds/captures without idempotency keys
- storing card data without strict tokenization and compliance controls
- treating processor webhooks as exactly-once and not deduplicating

## real_world_examples
- idempotent payment capture with dedupe keys and retry-safe handlers
- reconciliation jobs comparing processor settlement reports to internal ledger
