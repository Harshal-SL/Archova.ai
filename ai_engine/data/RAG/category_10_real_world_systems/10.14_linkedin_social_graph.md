# LinkedIn Social Graph

## chunk_id
- 10.14


## category
- real_world_systems

## type
- real_world_system

## definition
- a professional network system centered on graph relationships, feeds, search, and recommendations with strong identity and privacy needs.

## when_to_use
- modeling graph data access patterns with high fan-out reads
- building recommendation systems using connection and interaction signals
- designing privacy-aware visibility and search policies

## when_not_to_use
- simple profile directories with minimal graph features
- systems without strong privacy and identity constraints

## decision_signals
- graph traversal and feed reads dominate traffic (>80% reads)
- latency targets for graph queries and feed serving (<200ms)
- privacy rules require consistent enforcement across projections and search

## advantages
- graph-centric design enables rich recommendations and discovery
- projections allow scalable feeds and search over graph-derived data

## disadvantages
- graph skew creates hotspots around popular nodes
- privacy enforcement across derived datasets is complex

## anti_patterns
- computing recommendations synchronously in request paths
- duplicating graph-derived data without provenance and refresh strategy
- exposing search results without strict visibility filtering
