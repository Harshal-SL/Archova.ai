# Twitter Realtime Feed

## chunk_id
- 10.13


## category
- real_world_systems

## type
- real_world_system

## definition
- a real-time social feed system that ingests posts at high rate and serves timelines with low latency, ranking, and abuse controls.

## when_to_use
- designing fan-out and timeline generation strategies
- handling bursty writes and large follower graphs
- supporting near-real-time notifications and trends

## when_not_to_use
- small communities where timelines can be computed on demand
- systems that cannot tolerate eventual consistency in timelines

## decision_signals
- write throughput for posts and interactions (>10k events/s)
- timeline latency target (<200ms p95) with heavy caching
- follower graph skew creates hot keys and fan-out hotspots

## advantages
- clear separation of write path vs read projections for timelines
- streaming pipelines enable trends and realtime analytics

## disadvantages
- fan-out is expensive for large accounts and needs special handling
- abuse and spam defenses are continuous operational work

## anti_patterns
- using naive fan-out-on-write for extremely large follower accounts
- building timelines directly from transactional tables at scale
- retrying failed fan-out without caps and backpressure

## real_world_examples
- hybrid timeline: precomputed for most users + on-demand merge for heavy accounts
- event streaming for trends and notification pipelines
