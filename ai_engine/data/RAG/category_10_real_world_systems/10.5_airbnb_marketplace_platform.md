# Airbnb Marketplace Platform

## chunk_id
- 10.5


## category
- real_world_systems

## type
- real_world_system

## definition
- a two-sided marketplace system for listings, search, booking, payments, and trust workflows with high contention on inventory.

## when_to_use
- modeling booking holds, availability calendars, and cancellation rules
- designing search + ranking for listings with geo and filters
- handling fraud, reviews, and dispute workflows

## when_not_to_use
- simple catalogs with unlimited inventory
- low-volume booking without concurrency or trust requirements

## decision_signals
- inventory contention requires holds with expiration (<15 minutes typical)
- search latency needs <300ms with faceting and geo filters
- cancellation/refund workflows need compensations and auditability

## advantages
- clear domain decomposition (search, booking, payments, trust)
- event-driven workflows support asynchronous confirmations and updates

## disadvantages
- complex edge cases around holds, cancellations, and refunds
- trust and safety signals require continuous tuning and monitoring

## anti_patterns
- confirming bookings without atomic hold/commit semantics
- running search directly on transactional booking tables
- allowing unbounded retries for booking confirmation steps

## real_world_examples
- short-lived booking holds with payment confirmation windows
- dispute workflows with audit trails and evidence attachments
