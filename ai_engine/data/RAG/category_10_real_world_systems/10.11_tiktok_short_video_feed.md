# TikTok Short Video Feed

## chunk_id
- 10.11


## category
- real_world_systems

## type
- real_world_system

## definition
- a short-video system that delivers highly personalized feeds with heavy recommendation ranking and efficient global media delivery.

## when_to_use
- designing high-engagement feed ranking with rapid feedback loops
- combining media pipelines with low-latency discovery and caching
- handling bursty write traffic for likes, comments, and shares

## when_not_to_use
- content platforms without personalization-driven ranking
- small audiences where a simple timeline and storage are sufficient

## decision_signals
- personalization is the primary product lever with ranking latency budgets (<150ms)
- media delivery requires cdn offload and multi-bitrate encodes
- engagement events are high volume and must feed models continuously

## advantages
- strong separation between ranking systems and media delivery
- event-driven feedback loops improve recommendation quality over time

## disadvantages
- risks bias and feedback loops without governance and evaluation
- high cost for media storage, bandwidth, and model serving

## anti_patterns
- coupling feed delivery to synchronous model training jobs
- deploying ranking changes without offline evaluation and guardrails
- ignoring content safety and abuse signals in ranking inputs
