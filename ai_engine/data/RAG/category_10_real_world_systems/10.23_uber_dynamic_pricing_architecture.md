# Uber Dynamic Pricing Architecture

## chunk_id
- 10.23


## category
- real_world_systems

## type
- real_world_system

## definition
- a surge pricing architecture that combines demand signals, driver supply, and regional constraints to compute near-real-time multipliers safely.

## when_to_use
- marketplaces with volatile supply and demand imbalance
- pricing decisions that must react within seconds
- systems needing guardrails for fairness and compliance

## when_not_to_use
- fixed-price services with stable demand patterns
- low-scale systems where manual pricing is sufficient

## decision_signals
- need: marketplaces with volatile supply and demand imbalance
- need: pricing decisions that must react within seconds
- willing to accept: model drift and feedback-loop risk

## advantages
- improves marketplace liquidity during peak periods
- provides tunable controls by city, zone, and event context

## disadvantages
- model drift and feedback-loop risk
- user trust can degrade without transparent pricing behavior

## anti_patterns
- applying one multiplier globally across dissimilar zones
- using stale demand windows during rapid events
- ignoring rollback paths for pricing incidents

## real_world_examples
- zone-level surge multipliers updated from streaming demand features
- hard caps and cooldown periods to stabilize extreme events
