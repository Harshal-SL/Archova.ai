# Serverless Compute Platform


## chunk_id
- 7.21

## category
- infrastructure_components

## type
- infrastructure_component

## definition
- a managed compute platform that runs functions or containers on demand with automatic scaling and usage-based billing.

## when_to_use
- event-driven workloads and bursty traffic patterns
- reducing ops overhead for small teams
- running background tasks triggered by messages or schedules

## when_not_to_use
- latency-sensitive workloads affected by cold starts without mitigations
- long-running or stateful workloads that exceed platform limits

## decision_signals
- need: event-driven workloads and bursty traffic patterns
- need: reducing ops overhead for small teams
- willing to accept: platform limits and cold starts can impact latency

## advantages
- scales automatically and reduces idle cost
- decreases infrastructure management burden

## disadvantages
- platform limits and cold starts can impact latency
- observability and local testing can be more complex

## anti_patterns
- using this when latency-sensitive workloads affected by cold starts without mitigations
- using this when long-running or stateful workloads that exceed platform limits
- ignoring the downside that platform limits and cold starts can impact latency

## real_world_examples
- running an image resize function triggered by object storage uploads
- processing queue messages with serverless workers
