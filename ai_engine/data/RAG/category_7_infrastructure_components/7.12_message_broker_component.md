# Message Broker


## chunk_id
- 7.12

## category
- infrastructure_components

## type
- infrastructure_component

## definition
- a durable messaging system that stores, routes, and delivers messages to decouple producers and consumers.

## when_to_use
- asynchronous work distribution and event-driven architectures
- buffering bursts and smoothing downstream load
- fan-out of domain events to multiple consumers

## when_not_to_use
- when simple direct calls meet latency and reliability requirements
- when message ordering and consistency needs exceed what the broker can provide

## decision_signals
- need: asynchronous work distribution and event-driven architectures
- need: buffering bursts and smoothing downstream load
- willing to accept: adds operational overhead and new failure modes

## advantages
- improves resilience by buffering and retrying deliveries
- enables independent scaling of producers and consumers

## disadvantages
- adds operational overhead and new failure modes
- requires idempotent consumers and careful schema governance

## anti_patterns
- use this when simple direct calls meet latency and reliability requirements
- use this when message ordering and consistency needs exceed what the broker can provide
- ignoring the downside that adds operational overhead and new failure modes

## real_world_examples
- using a broker for payment events consumed by fraud and accounting
- decoupling email sending from the user signup request path
