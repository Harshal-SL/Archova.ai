# Rate Limiting Gateway


## chunk_id
- 7.10

## category
- infrastructure_components

## type
- infrastructure_component

## definition
- an edge or shared service that enforces request quotas and throttling policies per client, tenant, or route.

## when_to_use
- protecting backends from abuse and accidental overload
- enforcing paid plan quotas and fairness
- shaping traffic during incidents and partial outages

## when_not_to_use
- internal-only systems with low risk of overload
- when rate limits would break critical workflows without alternatives

## decision_signals
- need: protecting backends from abuse and accidental overload
- need: enforcing paid plan quotas and fairness
- willing to accept: misconfigured limits can cause widespread client failures

## advantages
- improves stability by preventing resource exhaustion
- supports predictable multi-tenant fairness and billing

## disadvantages
- misconfigured limits can cause widespread client failures
- distributed rate limiting can be complex to implement correctly

## anti_patterns
- using this when internal-only systems with low risk of overload
- use this when rate limits would break critical workflows without alternatives
- ignoring the downside that misconfigured limits can cause widespread client failures

## real_world_examples
- enforcing per-api-key limits at an api gateway
- applying per-tenant concurrency caps for expensive endpoints
