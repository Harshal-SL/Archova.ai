# Database Cluster


## chunk_id
- 7.13

## category
- infrastructure_components

## type
- infrastructure_component

## definition
- a database deployment composed of multiple nodes to provide replication, failover, and scalable capacity.

## when_to_use
- needing high availability and failover
- scaling reads with replicas or scaling storage with sharding
- meeting disaster recovery requirements

## when_not_to_use
- small single-node workloads with low availability needs
- when operational complexity is unacceptable for the team

## decision_signals
- need: needing high availability and failover
- need: scaling reads with replicas or scaling storage with sharding
- willing to accept: introduces consistency tradeoffs and replication lag

## advantages
- improves availability and supports growth
- enables read scaling and resilience to node failures

## disadvantages
- introduces consistency tradeoffs and replication lag
- requires monitoring, backups, and careful operational procedures

## anti_patterns
- using this when small single-node workloads with low availability needs
- use this when operational complexity is unacceptable for the team
- ignoring the downside that introduces consistency tradeoffs and replication lag

## real_world_examples
- primary with read replicas for low-latency reads
- sharded clusters for very large multi-tenant datasets
