# Data Pipeline and ETL


## chunk_id
- 7.24

## category
- infrastructure_components

## type
- infrastructure_component

## definition
- infrastructure for ingesting, transforming, and loading data into analytical stores with scheduling, retries, and lineage.

## when_to_use
- analytics, reporting, and data warehousing needs
- integrating data from multiple systems and formats
- enforcing data quality checks and lineage tracking

## when_not_to_use
- when operational databases already satisfy reporting requirements
- when data volume is small and a simple batch export is sufficient

## decision_signals
- need: analytics, reporting, and data warehousing needs
- need: integrating data from multiple systems and formats
- willing to accept: can become complex with many sources and transformations

## advantages
- enables reliable analytics and downstream consumption
- supports governance via lineage and quality checks

## disadvantages
- can become complex with many sources and transformations
- failures can produce partial or inconsistent analytical datasets

## anti_patterns
- use this when operational databases already satisfy reporting requirements
- use this when data volume is small and a simple batch export is sufficient
- ignoring the downside that can become complex with many sources and transformations

## real_world_examples
- streaming events into a warehouse with deduplication and late-event handling
- nightly etl to compute business kpis from transactional data
