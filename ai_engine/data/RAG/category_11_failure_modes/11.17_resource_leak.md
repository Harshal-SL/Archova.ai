# Resource Leak


## chunk_id
- 11.17

## category
- failure_modes

## type
- failure_mode

## definition
- a gradual loss of capacity caused by unreleased resources such as memory, file handles, threads, or database connections.

## when_to_use
- diagnosing slow degradation that resolves after restart
- systems using connection pools, background threads, or file io
- designing limits and monitoring for resource consumption

## when_not_to_use
- short-lived jobs that exit quickly and free resources by process termination
- systems with strong automatic resource management and proven leak-free behavior

## decision_signals
- need: diagnosing slow degradation that resolves after restart
- need: systems using connection pools, background threads, or file io
- willing to accept: can lead to sudden outages after long periods of apparent health

## advantages
- encourages tight limits, monitoring, and leak detection tooling
- improves operational readiness for long-lived services

## disadvantages
- can lead to sudden outages after long periods of apparent health
- root cause can be difficult to isolate in large codebases

## anti_patterns
- using this when short-lived jobs that exit quickly and free resources by process termination
- using this when systems with strong automatic resource management and proven leak-free behavior
- ignoring the downside that can lead to sudden outages after long periods of apparent health

## real_world_examples
- connections are not returned to the pool on certain error paths
- unbounded caches retain objects and increase memory until oom
