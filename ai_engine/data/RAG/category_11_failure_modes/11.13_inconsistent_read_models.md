# Inconsistent Read Models


## chunk_id
- 11.13

## category
- failure_modes

## type
- failure_mode

## definition
- read-side projections become incorrect or lag behind the write-side due to missed events, consumer lag, or buggy projection logic.

## when_to_use
- cqrs and event-driven architectures with projections
- debugging discrepancies between source of truth and views
- designing replay, backfill, and reconciliation processes

## when_not_to_use
- single-store designs where reads come directly from the primary database
- systems without asynchronous projection pipelines

## decision_signals
- need: cqrs and event-driven architectures with projections
- need: debugging discrepancies between source of truth and views
- willing to accept: users may see outdated or incorrect data temporarily

## advantages
- motivates idempotent projections, checkpoints, and replayability
- improves correctness through reconciliation and audits

## disadvantages
- users may see outdated or incorrect data temporarily
- fixing projection bugs may require large reprocessing jobs

## anti_patterns
- using this when single-store designs where reads come directly from the primary database
- using this when systems without asynchronous projection pipelines
- ignoring the downside that users may see outdated or incorrect data temporarily

## real_world_examples
- a consumer outage causes search index to lag behind catalog updates
- a projection bug drops certain event types and silently corrupts a view
