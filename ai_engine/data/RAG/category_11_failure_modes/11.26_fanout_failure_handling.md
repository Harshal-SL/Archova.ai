# Fanout Failure Handling


## chunk_id
- 11.26

## category
- failure_modes

## type
- failure_mode

## definition
- failure mode where one-to-many distribution paths partially fail, causing inconsistent recipient delivery and costly retries.

## when_to_use
- analyzing notification/feed delivery incidents
- designing retry and compensation for partial fanout
- building slos for large audience delivery systems

## when_not_to_use
- single-recipient workflows with no fanout behavior
- systems with strict transactional multicast guarantees

## decision_signals
- need: designing retry and compensation for partial fanout
- need: building slos for large audience delivery systems
- willing to accept: duplicate suppression and reconciliation overhead

## advantages
- encourages partition-aware retries and idempotent delivery
- improves observability for partial delivery states

## disadvantages
- duplicate suppression and reconciliation overhead
- silent partial failures can hide behind aggregate success metrics

## anti_patterns
- retrying full audience fanout for localized failures
- lacking per-segment success tracking
- treating producer ack as end-to-end delivery confirmation

## real_world_examples
- push notification job succeeds for 95% shards but fails for one region
- social feed fanout misses followers during transient broker outage
