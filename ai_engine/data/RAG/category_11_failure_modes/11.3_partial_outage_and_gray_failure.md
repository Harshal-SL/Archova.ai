# Partial Outage and Gray Failure


## chunk_id
- 11.3

## category
- failure_modes

## type
- failure_mode

## definition
- a condition where a service is partially failing (some requests succeed) due to degraded nodes, network issues, or intermittent errors.

## when_to_use
- designing health checks and load balancing strategies
- troubleshooting intermittent timeouts and elevated tail latency
- modeling resilience to flaky dependencies

## when_not_to_use
- systems with clear binary up/down behavior and strong failover
- purely batch jobs where partial success is not meaningful

## decision_signals
- need: designing health checks and load balancing strategies
- need: troubleshooting intermittent timeouts and elevated tail latency
- willing to accept: harder to detect and diagnose than full outages

## advantages
- encourages better probes, per-endpoint health, and adaptive routing
- improves incident detection beyond simple uptime

## disadvantages
- harder to detect and diagnose than full outages
- can trigger retry storms and cascading failures

## anti_patterns
- using this when systems with clear binary up/down behavior and strong failover
- using this when purely batch jobs where partial success is not meaningful
- ignoring the downside that harder to detect and diagnose than full outages

## real_world_examples
- one availability zone has packet loss causing intermittent timeouts
- a subset of pods has an exhausted connection pool causing sporadic failures
