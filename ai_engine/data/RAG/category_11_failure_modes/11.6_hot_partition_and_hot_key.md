# Hot Partition and Hot Key


## chunk_id
- 11.6

## category
- failure_modes

## type
- failure_mode

## definition
- uneven traffic concentrated on a single shard, partition, or key, causing localized overload while the cluster appears underutilized.

## when_to_use
- sharded databases, partitioned queues, and caches
- diagnosing high latency with low average utilization
- designing key schemes and load distribution

## when_not_to_use
- single-node storage with no partitioning
- workloads with naturally uniform key distribution

## decision_signals
- need: sharded databases, partitioned queues, and caches
- need: diagnosing high latency with low average utilization
- willing to accept: creates localized throttling and timeouts

## advantages
- highlights importance of key design, salting, and adaptive partitioning
- improves capacity planning for tail workloads

## disadvantages
- creates localized throttling and timeouts
- can trigger cascading retries and instability

## anti_patterns
- using this when single-node storage with no partitioning
- using this when workloads with naturally uniform key distribution
- ignoring the downside that creates localized throttling and timeouts

## real_world_examples
- one tenant_id dominates traffic and overloads a shard
- a popular item_id causes cache and database hotspots
