# Region Outage and Failover Failure


## chunk_id
- 11.25

## category
- failure_modes

## type
- failure_mode

## definition
- a major zone or region becomes unavailable, and failover mechanisms do not work as expected due to dependencies, state, or misconfiguration.

## when_to_use
- designing multi-region architectures and disaster recovery
- evaluating failover runbooks and data replication strategies
- testing resilience with chaos and game days

## when_not_to_use
- single-region systems with no dr requirements
- non-critical systems where downtime is acceptable

## decision_signals
- need: designing multi-region architectures and disaster recovery
- need: evaluating failover runbooks and data replication strategies
- willing to accept: multi-region designs increase complexity and cost

## advantages
- drives investment in clear rto/rpo targets and tested recovery
- reduces business risk from large infrastructure events

## disadvantages
- multi-region designs increase complexity and cost
- failovers can cause data loss or split-brain if not carefully managed

## anti_patterns
- using this when single-region systems with no dr requirements
- using this when non-critical systems where downtime is acceptable
- ignoring the downside that multi-region designs increase complexity and cost

## real_world_examples
- regional storage outage breaks both primary and failover due to shared dependency
- dns failover works, but databases cannot accept writes due to replication lag
