# Split-Brain


## chunk_id
- 11.4

## category
- failure_modes

## type
- failure_mode

## definition
- a distributed system condition where two or more partitions believe they are the primary and accept writes independently.

## when_to_use
- designing leader election and quorum-based replication
- evaluating network partition behavior and failover policies
- modeling consistency requirements for writes

## when_not_to_use
- single-node systems with no leader election
- read-only replicas that never accept writes

## decision_signals
- need: designing leader election and quorum-based replication
- need: evaluating network partition behavior and failover policies
- willing to accept: can cause data divergence and hard-to-reconcile conflicts

## advantages
- pushes designs toward quorum, fencing tokens, and safe failover
- clarifies tradeoffs between availability and consistency

## disadvantages
- can cause data divergence and hard-to-reconcile conflicts
- recovery may require manual intervention and data repair

## anti_patterns
- using this when single-node systems with no leader election
- using this when read-only replicas that never accept writes
- ignoring the downside that can cause data divergence and hard-to-reconcile conflicts

## real_world_examples
- network partition isolates datacenters and both accept writes
- stale leader continues writing after losing quorum due to clock issues
