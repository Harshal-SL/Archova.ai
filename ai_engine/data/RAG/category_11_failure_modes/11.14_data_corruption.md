# Data Corruption


## chunk_id
- 11.14

## category
- failure_modes

## type
- failure_mode

## definition
- unintended modification, truncation, or incorrect encoding of data that breaks invariants and produces incorrect outputs.

## when_to_use
- systems handling schema migrations and complex transformations
- diagnosing unexplained incorrect balances, missing records, or invalid states
- designing backup, validation, and repair workflows

## when_not_to_use
- ephemeral caches where data loss is acceptable and recoverable
- systems where all writes are strictly validated and append-only (risk is reduced, not eliminated)

## decision_signals
- need: systems handling schema migrations and complex transformations
- need: diagnosing unexplained incorrect balances, missing records, or invalid states
- willing to accept: can be hard to detect until downstream impact is large

## advantages
- drives adoption of checksums, invariants, and validation at boundaries
- motivates backup strategies and point-in-time recovery

## disadvantages
- can be hard to detect until downstream impact is large
- recovery may require complex backfills and manual corrections

## anti_patterns
- using this when ephemeral caches where data loss is acceptable and recoverable
- using this when systems where all writes are strictly validated and append-only (risk is reduced, not eliminated)
- ignoring the downside that can be hard to detect until downstream impact is large

## real_world_examples
- a bug writes wrong units (cents vs dollars) into a ledger
- an incorrect migration drops fields or maps enums incorrectly
