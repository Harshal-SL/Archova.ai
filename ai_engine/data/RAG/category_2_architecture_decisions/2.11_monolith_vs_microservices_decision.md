# Monolith vs Microservices Decision


## chunk_id
- 2.11

## category
- architecture_decisions

## type
- decision

## definition
- a decision guide for choosing a single deployable system (monolith) versus independently deployable services (microservices).

## when_to_use
- monolith for mvp delivery with small teams
- microservices for many teams needing independent deployments
- modular monolith as a stepping stone with clear boundaries

## when_not_to_use
- microservices when operational maturity and observability are low
- monolith when team autonomy and scaling needs are blocked

## decision_signals
- need: monolith for mvp delivery with small teams
- need: microservices for many teams needing independent deployments
- willing to accept: monolith can become tightly coupled and hard to scale by domain

## advantages
- monolith reduces operational overhead and coordination
- microservices enable independent scaling and fault isolation

## disadvantages
- monolith can become tightly coupled and hard to scale by domain
- microservices increase distributed systems complexity and cost

## anti_patterns
- using this when microservices when operational maturity and observability are low
- using this when monolith when team autonomy and scaling needs are blocked
- ignoring the downside that monolith can become tightly coupled and hard to scale by domain

## real_world_examples
- early-stage products starting as monoliths
- large marketplaces evolving into microservices
