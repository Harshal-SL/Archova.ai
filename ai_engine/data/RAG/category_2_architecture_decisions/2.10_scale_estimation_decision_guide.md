# Scale Estimation Decision Guide


## chunk_id
- 2.10

## category
- architecture_decisions

## type
- decision

## definition
- a sizing process estimating qps, storage, and bandwidth to pick appropriate architecture and capacity.

## when_to_use
- early architecture planning to choose databases, caches, and queues
- cost planning for multi-tenant and high-traffic services
- validating whether scaling techniques are required

## when_not_to_use
- tiny internal tools with stable low usage
- when assumptions are too uncertain to inform decisions

## decision_signals
- need: early architecture planning to choose databases, caches, and queues
- need: cost planning for multi-tenant and high-traffic services
- willing to accept: estimates can be wrong without real measurements

## advantages
- prevents under-provisioning and avoids premature over-design
- guides bottleneck identification and performance budgeting

## disadvantages
- estimates can be wrong without real measurements
- can consume time if taken beyond rough order-of-magnitude

## anti_patterns
- using this when tiny internal tools with stable low usage
- use this when assumptions are too uncertain to inform decisions
- ignoring the downside that estimates can be wrong without real measurements

## real_world_examples
- estimating feed read qps for caching strategy selection
- estimating upload bandwidth for object storage sizing
