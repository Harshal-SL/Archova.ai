# Strong vs Eventual Consistency


## chunk_id
- 2.1

## category
- architecture_decisions

## type
- decision

## definition
- a consistency tradeoff between immediate correctness (strong) and convergent correctness over time (eventual).

## when_to_use
- strong consistency for financial balances and inventory
- eventual consistency for feeds and analytics views
- hybrid consistency for user-facing reads with session guarantees

## when_not_to_use
- strong consistency when multi-region availability is the top priority
- eventual consistency when correctness errors are unacceptable

## decision_signals
- need: strong consistency for financial balances and inventory
- need: eventual consistency for feeds and analytics views
- willing to accept: strong consistency can increase latency under replication

## advantages
- strong consistency simplifies reasoning and invariants
- eventual consistency improves availability and geo-latency

## disadvantages
- strong consistency can increase latency under replication
- eventual consistency can produce stale reads and conflicts

## anti_patterns
- using this when strong consistency when multi-region availability is the top priority
- using this when eventual consistency when correctness errors are unacceptable
- ignoring the downside that strong consistency can increase latency under replication

## real_world_examples
- seat booking and payment authorization (strong)
- social timelines and counters (eventual)
