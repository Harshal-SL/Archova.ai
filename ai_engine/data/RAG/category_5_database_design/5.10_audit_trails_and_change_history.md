# Audit Trails and Change History


## chunk_id
- 5.10

## category
- database_design

## type
- data_pattern

## definition
- storing who changed what and when, using append-only logs, history tables, or event streams for traceability.

## when_to_use
- compliance requirements for immutable audit logs
- debugging complex business workflows and customer support
- reconstructing state at a point in time

## when_not_to_use
- when the data has no auditing requirements and storage costs are critical
- when audit data would capture sensitive fields without proper controls

## decision_signals
- need: compliance requirements for immutable audit logs
- need: debugging complex business workflows and customer support
- willing to accept: increases write volume and storage

## advantages
- improves accountability and incident investigations
- enables point-in-time analysis and rollback support

## disadvantages
- increases write volume and storage
- requires careful access controls and retention policies

## anti_patterns
- use this when the data has no auditing requirements and storage costs are critical
- use this when audit data would capture sensitive fields without proper controls
- ignoring the downside that increases write volume and storage

## real_world_examples
- writing audit records for permission changes and admin actions
- keeping an order status history for dispute resolution
