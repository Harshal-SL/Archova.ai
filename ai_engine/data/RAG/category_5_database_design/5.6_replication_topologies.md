# Replication Topologies


## chunk_id
- 5.6

## category
- database_design

## type
- data_pattern

## definition
- selecting replication modes (single-leader, multi-leader, leaderless) to balance consistency, availability, and write locality.

## when_to_use
- improving read availability with replicas
- supporting multi-region reads and disaster recovery
- deciding how to handle regional writes and failover

## when_not_to_use
- when the system cannot tolerate conflicts or stale reads
- when operational complexity outweighs the reliability needs

## decision_signals
- need: improving read availability with replicas
- need: supporting multi-region reads and disaster recovery
- willing to accept: introduces replication lag and consistency tradeoffs

## advantages
- increases availability and resilience to node failures
- can reduce read latency by serving from local replicas

## disadvantages
- introduces replication lag and consistency tradeoffs
- multi-leader and leaderless setups require conflict resolution

## anti_patterns
- use this when the system cannot tolerate conflicts or stale reads
- use this when operational complexity outweighs the reliability needs
- ignoring the downside that introduces replication lag and consistency tradeoffs

## real_world_examples
- primary in one region with read replicas in others for low-latency reads
- multi-leader replication for active-active regional writes with conflict policies
